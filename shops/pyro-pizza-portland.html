Pyro Pizza

Home: Portland, Oregon
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled, cart-born pies
Signature pie: Meatball pie

The record of Wood-fired pizza in OR runs through rooms like Pyro Pizza in Portland, where the meatball pie is treated as the main event rather than an option.

Pyro Pizza works to that standard in Portland — and asks to be judged by it. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. In Portland, Pyro Pizza would not serve it any other way.

Here the meatball is crumbled, cart-born pies. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. In Portland, Pyro Pizza would not serve it any other way.

Order Meatball pie — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. That is how Pyro Pizza keeps it in Portland.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. In Portland, Pyro Pizza would not serve it any other way.
