Fong's Pizza

Home: Des Moines, Iowa
Style: New York slice
The meatball: crumbled with crab rangoon fame
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Fong's Pizza works in the New York slice tradition in Des Moines, IA, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

That is the bar Fong's Pizza sets for itself in Des Moines, pie after pie.
