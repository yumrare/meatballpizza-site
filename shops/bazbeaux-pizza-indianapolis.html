Bazbeaux Pizza

Home: Indianapolis, Indiana
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

In Indianapolis, IN, the meatball pizza at Bazbeaux Pizza is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a New York slice pie judged by its hardest topping.

At Bazbeaux Pizza in Indianapolis, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. That is how Bazbeaux Pizza keeps it in Indianapolis.

The meatball itself comes sliced: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. Indianapolis has other pies; Bazbeaux Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. That is how Bazbeaux Pizza keeps it in Indianapolis.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. That is how Bazbeaux Pizza keeps it in Indianapolis.
