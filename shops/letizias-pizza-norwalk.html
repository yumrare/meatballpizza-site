Letizia's Pizza

Home: Norwalk, Connecticut
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, Fairfield County classic
Signature pie: Meatball slice

The directory files Letizia's Pizza of Norwalk, CT under New York slice — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball slice.

It is the standard Letizia's Pizza was built on in Norwalk, and the one its regulars enforce. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Letizia's Pizza keeps that standard in Norwalk, night after night.

The meatball itself comes sliced, Fairfield County classic: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. That is how Letizia's Pizza keeps it in Norwalk.

Order Meatball slice — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. In Norwalk, Letizia's Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. Letizia's Pizza keeps that standard in Norwalk, night after night.

That is the whole of the Letizia's Pizza entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
