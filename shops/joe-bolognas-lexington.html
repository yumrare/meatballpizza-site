Joe Bologna's

Home: Lexington, Kentucky
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced thin on a blistered crust
Signature pie: Meatball slice

A meatball forgives nothing, and Joe Bologna's in Lexington, KY builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a New York slice crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

Joe Bologna's in Lexington is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Joe Bologna's keeps that standard in Lexington, night after night.

The preparation is sliced thin on a blistered crust. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. Lexington has other pies; Joe Bologna's stakes its name on this one.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. In Lexington, Joe Bologna's would not serve it any other way.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. Lexington has other pies; Joe Bologna's stakes its name on this one.

Joe Bologna's is filed in the New York slice chapter, under Lexington, KY. Menus change; the record keeps the signature as it stands.
