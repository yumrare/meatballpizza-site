Hard Knox Pizza

Home: Knoxville, Tennessee
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Hard Knox Pizza (Knoxville, TN) keeps the Wood-fired faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

Hard Knox Pizza in Knoxville is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. Knoxville has other pies; Hard Knox Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. Hard Knox Pizza keeps that standard in Knoxville, night after night.

Meatball pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. Hard Knox Pizza keeps that standard in Knoxville, night after night.
