Federici's of Freehold Boro

Home: Freehold, New Jersey
Style: Bar pie
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball bar pie

Federici's of Freehold Boro is Freehold's entry in the meatball pizza record — a Bar pie kitchen in NJ where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

Federici's of Freehold Boro works to that standard in Freehold — and asks to be judged by it. The preparation is sliced. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. Federici's of Freehold Boro keeps that standard in Freehold, night after night.
