Blue Pan Pizza

Home: Denver, Colorado
Style: Detroit style
The meatball: crumbled on award-winning squares
Signature pie: Meatball square

A meatball forgives nothing, and Blue Pan Pizza in Denver, CO builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a Detroit style crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

It is the standard Blue Pan Pizza was built on in Denver, and the one its regulars enforce. Detroit style bakes in blue-steel pans to a frico edge — cheese crisped against the metal all the way around — with toppings carried to the very corner. That is how Blue Pan Pizza keeps it in Denver.

The meatball itself comes crumbled on award-winning squares: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. It is the Blue Pan Pizza way in Denver, and the regulars hold them to it.

Order Meatball square — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. Blue Pan Pizza keeps that standard in Denver, night after night.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. In Denver, Blue Pan Pizza would not serve it any other way.

Blue Pan Pizza is filed in the Detroit style chapter, under Denver, CO. Menus change; the record keeps the signature as it stands.
