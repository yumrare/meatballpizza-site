Geraci's Restaurant

Home: University Heights, Ohio
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced thin on a blistered crust
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Geraci's Restaurant works in the New York slice tradition in University Heights, OH, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

Geraci's Restaurant works to that standard in University Heights — and asks to be judged by it. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. In University Heights, Geraci's Restaurant would not serve it any other way.

The preparation is sliced thin on a blistered crust. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them.
