Sammy's Pizza

Home: Duluth, Minnesota
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled, house style
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

Sammy's Pizza (Duluth, MN) keeps the Chicago tavern thin faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

That is the bar Sammy's Pizza sets for itself in Duluth, pie after pie. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. Duluth has other pies; Sammy's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is crumbled, house style — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. It is the Sammy's Pizza way in Duluth, and the regulars hold them to it.

The pie to know is Meatball tavern pie. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. It is the Sammy's Pizza way in Duluth, and the regulars hold them to it.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. Sammy's Pizza keeps that standard in Duluth, night after night.

Sammy's Pizza earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
