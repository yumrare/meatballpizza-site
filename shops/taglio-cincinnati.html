Cincinnati, Ohio · Directory Entry

Taglio

Home: Cincinnati, Ohio
Style: Detroit style
The meatball: crumbled on Detroit squares
Signature pie: Meatball square

Taglio is Cincinnati's entry in the meatball pizza record — a Detroit style kitchen in OH where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

Regulars in Cincinnati hold Taglio to exactly that standard. Detroit style bakes in blue-steel pans to a frico edge — cheese crisped against the metal all the way around — with toppings carried to the very corner. That is how Taglio keeps it in Cincinnati.

The preparation is crumbled on Detroit squares — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. That is how Taglio keeps it in Cincinnati.

Meatball square is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. It is the Taglio way in Cincinnati, and the regulars hold them to it.

Shops like this one hold a neighborhood together: the birthday pie, the Friday pie, the pie after the game. The meatball version is the connoisseur's standing order among them. Taglio keeps that standard in Cincinnati, night after night.

The Taglio kitchen in Cincinnati treats this as doctrine. Why does the meatball pie matter enough to anchor a national record? Because it grades everything at once. The grind shows in the texture. The seasoning shows in the first bite. The breadcrumb and the milk — or their absence — show in the tenderness. A kitchen can hide behind a fashionable crust or a rare cheese; it cannot hide behind a meatball.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. Taglio is filed under Cincinnati, OH, in the Detroit style chapter.
