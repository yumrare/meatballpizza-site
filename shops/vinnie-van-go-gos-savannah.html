Vinnie Van Go-Go's

Home: Savannah, Georgia
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, City Market slices
Signature pie: Meatball slice

The directory files Vinnie Van Go-Go's of Savannah, GA under New York slice — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball slice.

At Vinnie Van Go-Go's in Savannah, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. That is how Vinnie Van Go-Go's keeps it in Savannah.

The preparation is sliced, City Market slices. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. It is the Vinnie Van Go-Go's way in Savannah, and the regulars hold them to it.
