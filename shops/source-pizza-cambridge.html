Cambridge, Massachusetts · Directory Entry

Source Pizza

Home: Cambridge, Massachusetts
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Ask regulars in Cambridge where the meatball pizza matters and Source Pizza comes up — a Wood-fired shop whose signature, Meatball pie, earned this page.

At Source Pizza in Cambridge, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. It is the Source Pizza way in Cambridge, and the regulars hold them to it.

Here the meatball is crumbled. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. Source Pizza keeps that standard in Cambridge, night after night.

Meatball pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. That is how Source Pizza keeps it in Cambridge.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. Cambridge has other pies; Source Pizza stakes its name on this one.

Source Pizza is filed in the Wood-fired chapter, under Cambridge, MA. Menus change; the record keeps the signature as it stands.
