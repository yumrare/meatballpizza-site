Zee's Pizzeria

Home: New Orleans, Louisiana
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, Uptown
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Ask regulars in New Orleans where the meatball pizza matters and Zee's Pizzeria comes up — a New York slice shop whose signature, Meatball slice, earned this page.

That is the bar Zee's Pizzeria sets for itself in New Orleans, pie after pie. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. New Orleans has other pies; Zee's Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is sliced, Uptown. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. New Orleans has other pies; Zee's Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Zee's Pizzeria keeps that standard in New Orleans, night after night.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. That is how Zee's Pizzeria keeps it in New Orleans.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. Zee's Pizzeria is filed under New Orleans, LA, in the New York slice chapter.
