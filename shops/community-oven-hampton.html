Community Oven

Home: Hampton, New Hampshire
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Ask regulars in Hampton where the meatball pizza matters and Community Oven comes up — a Wood-fired shop whose signature, Meatball pie, earned this page.

Regulars in Hampton hold Community Oven to exactly that standard. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. Community Oven keeps that standard in Hampton, night after night.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. Hampton has other pies; Community Oven stakes its name on this one.

Order Meatball pie — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. Community Oven keeps that standard in Hampton, night after night.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. It is the Community Oven way in Hampton, and the regulars hold them to it.

Community Oven earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
