Bagby Pizza

Home: Baltimore, Maryland
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced downtown
Signature pie: Meatball slice

In Baltimore, MD, the meatball pizza at Bagby Pizza is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a New York slice pie judged by its hardest topping.

It is the standard Bagby Pizza was built on in Baltimore, and the one its regulars enforce. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. In Baltimore, Bagby Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The preparation is sliced downtown. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. In Baltimore, Bagby Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Baltimore has other pies; Bagby Pizza stakes its name on this one.
