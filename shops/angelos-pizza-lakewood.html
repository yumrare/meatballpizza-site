Angelo's Pizza

Home: Lakewood, Ohio
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, Lakewood institution
Signature pie: Meatball slice

A meatball forgives nothing, and Angelo's Pizza in Lakewood, OH builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a New York slice crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

Angelo's Pizza in Lakewood is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Lakewood has other pies; Angelo's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is sliced, Lakewood institution. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. Lakewood has other pies; Angelo's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

Meatball slice is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. That is how Angelo's Pizza keeps it in Lakewood.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. Angelo's Pizza keeps that standard in Lakewood, night after night.
