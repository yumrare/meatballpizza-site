Santa Fe, New Mexico · Directory Entry

Upper Crust Pizza

Home: Santa Fe, New Mexico
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced thin on a blistered crust
Signature pie: Meatball slice

The directory files Upper Crust Pizza of Santa Fe, NM under New York slice — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball slice.

At Upper Crust Pizza in Santa Fe, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Upper Crust Pizza keeps that standard in Santa Fe, night after night.

The meatball itself comes sliced thin on a blistered crust: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. Upper Crust Pizza keeps that standard in Santa Fe, night after night.

Order Meatball slice — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. In Santa Fe, Upper Crust Pizza would not serve it any other way.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. It is the Upper Crust Pizza way in Santa Fe, and the regulars hold them to it.

That is the whole of the Upper Crust Pizza entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
