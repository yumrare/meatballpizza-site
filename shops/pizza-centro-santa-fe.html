Pizza Centro

Home: Santa Fe, New Mexico
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Ask regulars in Santa Fe where the meatball pizza matters and Pizza Centro comes up — a New York slice shop whose signature, Meatball slice, earned this page.

Regulars in Santa Fe hold Pizza Centro to exactly that standard. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. In Santa Fe, Pizza Centro would not serve it any other way.

The preparation is sliced. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. Pizza Centro keeps that standard in Santa Fe, night after night.

Order Meatball slice — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. It is the Pizza Centro way in Santa Fe, and the regulars hold them to it.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. Pizza Centro keeps that standard in Santa Fe, night after night.
