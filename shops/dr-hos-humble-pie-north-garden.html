Dr. Ho's Humble Pie

Home: North Garden, Virginia
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled, Albemarle farm pies
Signature pie: Meatball pie

In North Garden, VA, the meatball pizza at Dr. Ho's Humble Pie is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a Wood-fired pie judged by its hardest topping.

At Dr. Ho's Humble Pie in North Garden, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. North Garden has other pies; Dr. Ho's Humble Pie stakes its name on this one.

Here the meatball is crumbled, Albemarle farm pies. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. It is the Dr. Ho's Humble Pie way in North Garden, and the regulars hold them to it.
