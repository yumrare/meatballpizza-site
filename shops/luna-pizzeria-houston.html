Luna Pizzeria

Home: Houston, Texas
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Luna Pizzeria is Houston's entry in the meatball pizza record — a New York slice kitchen in TX where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

It is the standard Luna Pizzeria was built on in Houston, and the one its regulars enforce. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. That is how Luna Pizzeria keeps it in Houston.

Here the meatball is sliced — sliced before it meets the pie, which is the pizzeria's oldest trick for even cooking: no raw centers, no rolling cargo, just meat that crisps where the oven can reach it. That is how Luna Pizzeria keeps it in Houston.

Meatball slice is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. Houston has other pies; Luna Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. In Houston, Luna Pizzeria would not serve it any other way.

That is the whole of the Luna Pizzeria entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
