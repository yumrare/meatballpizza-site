Galleria Umberto

Home: Boston, Massachusetts
Style: Sicilian
The meatball: crumbled over lunch-only squares
Signature pie: Meatball square

Galleria Umberto works in the Sicilian tradition in Boston, MA, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

Galleria Umberto works to that standard in Boston — and asks to be judged by it. The Sicilian square is a bakery pie: a thick, olive-oil-crisped crust, sauce over or under the cheese depending on the house, and corners that regulars request by name. In Boston, Galleria Umberto would not serve it any other way.

The meatball itself comes crumbled over lunch-only squares: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. Galleria Umberto keeps that standard in Boston, night after night.

Meatball square is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. In Boston, Galleria Umberto would not serve it any other way.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. Boston has other pies; Galleria Umberto stakes its name on this one.

Galleria Umberto earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
