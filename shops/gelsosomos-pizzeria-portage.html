Gelsosomo's Pizzeria

Home: Portage, Indiana
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

Ask regulars in Portage where the meatball pizza matters and Gelsosomo's Pizzeria comes up — a Chicago tavern thin shop whose signature, Meatball tavern pie, earned this page.

It is the standard Gelsosomo's Pizzeria was built on in Portage, and the one its regulars enforce. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. That is how Gelsosomo's Pizzeria keeps it in Portage.

Here the meatball is crumbled. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. Portage has other pies; Gelsosomo's Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.
