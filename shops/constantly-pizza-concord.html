Constantly Pizza

Home: Concord, New Hampshire
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

A meatball forgives nothing, and Constantly Pizza in Concord, NH builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a New York slice crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

Constantly Pizza works to that standard in Concord — and asks to be judged by it. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. That is how Constantly Pizza keeps it in Concord.

The meatball itself comes sliced: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. That is how Constantly Pizza keeps it in Concord.

The pie to know is Meatball slice. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. It is the Constantly Pizza way in Concord, and the regulars hold them to it.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. It is the Constantly Pizza way in Concord, and the regulars hold them to it.
