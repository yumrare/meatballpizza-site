Tribute Pizza

Home: San Diego, California
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled, North Park homages
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Tribute Pizza is San Diego's entry in the meatball pizza record — a Wood-fired kitchen in CA where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

Regulars in San Diego hold Tribute Pizza to exactly that standard. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. Tribute Pizza keeps that standard in San Diego, night after night.

The preparation is crumbled, North Park homages — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. San Diego has other pies; Tribute Pizza stakes its name on this one.

Order Meatball pie — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. San Diego has other pies; Tribute Pizza stakes its name on this one.

Shops like this one hold a neighborhood together: the birthday pie, the Friday pie, the pie after the game. The meatball version is the connoisseur's standing order among them. That is how Tribute Pizza keeps it in San Diego.
