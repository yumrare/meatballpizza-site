Rhombus Guys

Home: Fargo, North Dakota
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced with rooftop pies
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Rhombus Guys works in the New York slice tradition in Fargo, ND, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

Rhombus Guys in Fargo is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. In Fargo, Rhombus Guys would not serve it any other way.

The preparation is sliced with rooftop pies. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. In Fargo, Rhombus Guys would not serve it any other way.

The pie to know is Meatball slice. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. Fargo has other pies; Rhombus Guys stakes its name on this one.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. It is the Rhombus Guys way in Fargo, and the regulars hold them to it.

Rhombus Guys earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
