The Walnut Room

Home: Denver, Colorado
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled, live-music pies
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

The Walnut Room works in the Chicago tavern thin tradition in Denver, CO, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

The Walnut Room in Denver is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. Denver has other pies; The Walnut Room stakes its name on this one.

The meatball itself comes crumbled, live-music pies: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. In Denver, The Walnut Room would not serve it any other way.

Order Meatball tavern pie — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. It is the The Walnut Room way in Denver, and the regulars hold them to it.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. Denver has other pies; The Walnut Room stakes its name on this one.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. The Walnut Room is filed under Denver, CO, in the Chicago tavern thin chapter.
