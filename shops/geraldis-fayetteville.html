Geraldi's

Home: Fayetteville, Arkansas
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Geraldi's is Fayetteville's entry in the meatball pizza record — a New York slice kitchen in AR where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

It is the standard Geraldi's was built on in Fayetteville, and the one its regulars enforce. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. In Fayetteville, Geraldi's would not serve it any other way.

Here the meatball is sliced — sliced before it meets the pie, which is the pizzeria's oldest trick for even cooking: no raw centers, no rolling cargo, just meat that crisps where the oven can reach it. It is the Geraldi's way in Fayetteville, and the regulars hold them to it.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. In Fayetteville, Geraldi's would not serve it any other way.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. Geraldi's keeps that standard in Fayetteville, night after night.

Geraldi's earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
