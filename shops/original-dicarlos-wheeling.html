Original DiCarlo's

Home: Wheeling, West Virginia
Style: Ohio Valley style
The meatball: crumbled under cold provolone
Signature pie: Meatball square

Ask regulars in Wheeling where the meatball pizza matters and Original DiCarlo's comes up — a Ohio Valley style shop whose signature, Meatball square, earned this page.

That is the bar Original DiCarlo's sets for itself in Wheeling, pie after pie. Ohio Valley style bakes the crust and sauce first, then buries the hot pie under cold provolone and toppings after the oven — a bite that is hot and cold at once. It is the Original DiCarlo's way in Wheeling, and the regulars hold them to it.

Here the meatball is crumbled under cold provolone. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. That is how Original DiCarlo's keeps it in Wheeling.

The signature is Meatball square. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. In Wheeling, Original DiCarlo's would not serve it any other way.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. In Wheeling, Original DiCarlo's would not serve it any other way.

That is the whole of the Original DiCarlo's entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
