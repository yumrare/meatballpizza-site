Il Focolare

Home: Columbia, South Carolina
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

The record of Wood-fired pizza in SC runs through rooms like Il Focolare in Columbia, where the meatball pie is treated as the main event rather than an option.

Regulars in Columbia hold Il Focolare to exactly that standard. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. That is how Il Focolare keeps it in Columbia.

Here the meatball is crumbled. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. In Columbia, Il Focolare would not serve it any other way.

The signature is Meatball pie. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. That is how Il Focolare keeps it in Columbia.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. It is the Il Focolare way in Columbia, and the regulars hold them to it.

Il Focolare earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
