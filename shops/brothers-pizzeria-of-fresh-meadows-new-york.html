Brothers Pizzeria of Fresh Meadows

Home: New York, New York
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Brothers Pizzeria of Fresh Meadows works in the New York slice tradition in New York, NY, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

It is the standard Brothers Pizzeria of Fresh Meadows was built on in New York, and the one its regulars enforce. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. It is the Brothers Pizzeria of Fresh Meadows way in New York, and the regulars hold them to it.

The meatball itself comes sliced: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. New York has other pies; Brothers Pizzeria of Fresh Meadows stakes its name on this one.

Order Meatball slice — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. New York has other pies; Brothers Pizzeria of Fresh Meadows stakes its name on this one.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. In New York, Brothers Pizzeria of Fresh Meadows would not serve it any other way.
