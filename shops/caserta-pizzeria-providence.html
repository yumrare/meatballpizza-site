Caserta Pizzeria

Home: Providence, Rhode Island
Style: Sicilian
The meatball: crumbled over the Wimpy Skippy's house
Signature pie: Meatball square

In Providence, RI, the meatball pizza at Caserta Pizzeria is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a Sicilian pie judged by its hardest topping.

Regulars in Providence hold Caserta Pizzeria to exactly that standard. The Sicilian square is a bakery pie: a thick, olive-oil-crisped crust, sauce over or under the cheese depending on the house, and corners that regulars request by name. Providence has other pies; Caserta Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.

Here the meatball is crumbled over the Wimpy Skippy's house. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. In Providence, Caserta Pizzeria would not serve it any other way.

The signature is Meatball square. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Caserta Pizzeria keeps that standard in Providence, night after night.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. That is how Caserta Pizzeria keeps it in Providence.

Caserta Pizzeria earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
