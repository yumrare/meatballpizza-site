Shotgun Dan's Pizza

Home: Little Rock, Arkansas
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

In Little Rock, AR, the meatball pizza at Shotgun Dan's Pizza is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a New York slice pie judged by its hardest topping.

It is the standard Shotgun Dan's Pizza was built on in Little Rock, and the one its regulars enforce. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. In Little Rock, Shotgun Dan's Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The preparation is sliced. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. That is how Shotgun Dan's Pizza keeps it in Little Rock.

The pie to know is Meatball slice. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. Little Rock has other pies; Shotgun Dan's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. It is the Shotgun Dan's Pizza way in Little Rock, and the regulars hold them to it.
