Cosa Buona

Home: Los Angeles, California
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled, Echo Park red sauce pies
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Ask regulars in Los Angeles where the meatball pizza matters and Cosa Buona comes up — a Wood-fired shop whose signature, Meatball pie, earned this page.

Regulars in Los Angeles hold Cosa Buona to exactly that standard. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. Los Angeles has other pies; Cosa Buona stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is crumbled, Echo Park red sauce pies — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. It is the Cosa Buona way in Los Angeles, and the regulars hold them to it.
