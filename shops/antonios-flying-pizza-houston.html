Antonio's Flying Pizza

Home: Houston, Texas
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced thin on a blistered crust
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Ask regulars in Houston where the meatball pizza matters and Antonio's Flying Pizza comes up — a New York slice shop whose signature, Meatball slice, earned this page.

Antonio's Flying Pizza works to that standard in Houston — and asks to be judged by it. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Antonio's Flying Pizza keeps that standard in Houston, night after night.

The preparation is sliced thin on a blistered crust. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. Houston has other pies; Antonio's Flying Pizza stakes its name on this one.

Order Meatball slice — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. That is how Antonio's Flying Pizza keeps it in Houston.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. That is how Antonio's Flying Pizza keeps it in Houston.

That is the whole of the Antonio's Flying Pizza entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
