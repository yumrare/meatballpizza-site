Buck's Pizza

Home: Mobile, Alabama
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

The record of New York slice pizza in AL runs through rooms like Buck's Pizza in Mobile, where the meatball pie is treated as the main event rather than an option.

Regulars in Mobile hold Buck's Pizza to exactly that standard. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. That is how Buck's Pizza keeps it in Mobile.

The preparation is sliced. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. It is the Buck's Pizza way in Mobile, and the regulars hold them to it.

Order Meatball slice — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. Buck's Pizza keeps that standard in Mobile, night after night.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. Mobile has other pies; Buck's Pizza stakes its name on this one.
