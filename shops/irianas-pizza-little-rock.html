Iriana's Pizza

Home: Little Rock, Arkansas
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced downtown
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Iriana's Pizza is Little Rock's entry in the meatball pizza record — a New York slice kitchen in AR where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

At Iriana's Pizza in Little Rock, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. In Little Rock, Iriana's Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The preparation is sliced downtown. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. That is how Iriana's Pizza keeps it in Little Rock.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. That is how Iriana's Pizza keeps it in Little Rock.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. Iriana's Pizza keeps that standard in Little Rock, night after night.

That is the whole of the Iriana's Pizza entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
