Joe's Pizza

Home: Kansas City, Missouri
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, Westport by-the-slice
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Joe's Pizza is Kansas City's entry in the meatball pizza record — a New York slice kitchen in MO where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

At Joe's Pizza in Kansas City, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. That is how Joe's Pizza keeps it in Kansas City.

The preparation is sliced, Westport by-the-slice. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. Joe's Pizza keeps that standard in Kansas City, night after night.

Order Meatball slice — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. Kansas City has other pies; Joe's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. Joe's Pizza keeps that standard in Kansas City, night after night.

Joe's Pizza earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
