Oregano's Pizza Bistro

Home: Scottsdale, Arizona
Style: Chicago deep dish
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball deep dish

Oregano's Pizza Bistro (Scottsdale, AZ) keeps the Chicago deep dish faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

Oregano's Pizza Bistro in Scottsdale is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. Deep dish is a construction, not a crust with ambitions: a buttery pan walls in layers of cheese and filling under chunky tomato, and it is eaten with a knife and fork without apology. In Scottsdale, Oregano's Pizza Bistro would not serve it any other way.

The preparation is crumbled — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. Scottsdale has other pies; Oregano's Pizza Bistro stakes its name on this one.

Order Meatball deep dish — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. That is how Oregano's Pizza Bistro keeps it in Scottsdale.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. Oregano's Pizza Bistro keeps that standard in Scottsdale, night after night.
