La Rosa Pizzeria

Home: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

A meatball forgives nothing, and La Rosa Pizzeria in Philadelphia, PA builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a New York slice crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

That is the bar La Rosa Pizzeria sets for itself in Philadelphia, pie after pie. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. In Philadelphia, La Rosa Pizzeria would not serve it any other way.

Here the meatball is sliced — sliced before it meets the pie, which is the pizzeria's oldest trick for even cooking: no raw centers, no rolling cargo, just meat that crisps where the oven can reach it. That is how La Rosa Pizzeria keeps it in Philadelphia.

The pie to know is Meatball slice. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. La Rosa Pizzeria keeps that standard in Philadelphia, night after night.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. Philadelphia has other pies; La Rosa Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.
