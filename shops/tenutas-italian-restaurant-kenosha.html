Tenuta's Italian Restaurant

Home: Kenosha, Wisconsin
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

Tenuta's Italian Restaurant works in the Chicago tavern thin tradition in Kenosha, WI, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

Tenuta's Italian Restaurant works to that standard in Kenosha — and asks to be judged by it. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. Tenuta's Italian Restaurant keeps that standard in Kenosha, night after night.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps.
