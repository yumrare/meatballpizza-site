Pizzeria Caldera

Home: Jackson, Wyoming
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

The record of Neapolitan pizza in WY runs through rooms like Pizzeria Caldera in Jackson, where the meatball pie is treated as the main event rather than an option.

That is the bar Pizzeria Caldera sets for itself in Jackson, pie after pie. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. Pizzeria Caldera keeps that standard in Jackson, night after night.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. It is the Pizzeria Caldera way in Jackson, and the regulars hold them to it.
