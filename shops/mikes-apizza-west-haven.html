Mike's Apizza

Home: West Haven, Connecticut
Style: New Haven apizza
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball apizza

Ask regulars in West Haven where the meatball pizza matters and Mike's Apizza comes up — a New Haven apizza shop whose signature, Meatball apizza, earned this page.

Mike's Apizza in West Haven is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. Apizza — New Haven's dialect is New Haven's dialect — coal-fired, charred at the edges, tomato-forward, with pecorino's salt over mozzarella's pull. It is pronounced 'abeetz' and defended like a hometown team. Mike's Apizza keeps that standard in West Haven, night after night.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. In West Haven, Mike's Apizza would not serve it any other way.

The pie to know is Meatball apizza. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. West Haven has other pies; Mike's Apizza stakes its name on this one.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. Mike's Apizza keeps that standard in West Haven, night after night.
