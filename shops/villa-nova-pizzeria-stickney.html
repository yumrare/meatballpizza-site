Villa Nova Pizzeria

Home: Stickney, Illinois
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled, house style
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

Ask regulars in Stickney where the meatball pizza matters and Villa Nova Pizzeria comes up — a Chicago tavern thin shop whose signature, Meatball tavern pie, earned this page.

It is the standard Villa Nova Pizzeria was built on in Stickney, and the one its regulars enforce. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. Villa Nova Pizzeria keeps that standard in Stickney, night after night.

The meatball itself comes crumbled, house style: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. That is how Villa Nova Pizzeria keeps it in Stickney.

Meatball tavern pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. Villa Nova Pizzeria keeps that standard in Stickney, night after night.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. Stickney has other pies; Villa Nova Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.

That is the whole of the Villa Nova Pizzeria entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
