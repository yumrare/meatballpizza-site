Duane's House of Pizza

Home: Fargo, North Dakota
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled, house style
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

Ask regulars in Fargo where the meatball pizza matters and Duane's House of Pizza comes up — a Chicago tavern thin shop whose signature, Meatball tavern pie, earned this page.

Regulars in Fargo hold Duane's House of Pizza to exactly that standard. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. Fargo has other pies; Duane's House of Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is crumbled, house style — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. Fargo has other pies; Duane's House of Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The signature is Meatball tavern pie. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. In Fargo, Duane's House of Pizza would not serve it any other way.
