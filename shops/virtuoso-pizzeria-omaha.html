Virtuoso Pizzeria

Home: Omaha, Nebraska
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled, Benson
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Virtuoso Pizzeria works in the Neapolitan tradition in Omaha, NE, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

It is the standard Virtuoso Pizzeria was built on in Omaha, and the one its regulars enforce. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. It is the Virtuoso Pizzeria way in Omaha, and the regulars hold them to it.

Here the meatball is crumbled, Benson. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. Omaha has other pies; Virtuoso Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.

Order Meatball pie — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. Virtuoso Pizzeria keeps that standard in Omaha, night after night.

Shops like this one hold a neighborhood together: the birthday pie, the Friday pie, the pie after the game. The meatball version is the connoisseur's standing order among them. It is the Virtuoso Pizzeria way in Omaha, and the regulars hold them to it.
