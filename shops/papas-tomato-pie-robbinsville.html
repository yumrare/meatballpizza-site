Papa's Tomato Pie

Home: Robbinsville, New Jersey
Style: Tomato pie
The meatball: sliced over mustard-pie fame
Signature pie: Meatball tomato pie
On the record since: 1912

Papa's Tomato Pie is Robbinsville's entry in the meatball pizza record — a Tomato pie kitchen in NJ where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

Papa's Tomato Pie in Robbinsville is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The tomato pie inverts the usual order — a light hand with cheese under a bright, chunky tomato layer — a Trenton and South Philadelphia creed. In Robbinsville, Papa's Tomato Pie would not serve it any other way.

The meatball itself comes sliced over mustard-pie fame: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. Robbinsville has other pies; Papa's Tomato Pie stakes its name on this one.

The Papa's Tomato Pie kitchen in Robbinsville treats this as doctrine. Why does the meatball pie matter enough to anchor a national record? Because it grades everything at once. The grind shows in the texture. The seasoning shows in the first bite. The breadcrumb and the milk — or their absence — show in the tenderness. A kitchen can hide behind a fashionable crust or a rare cheese; it cannot hide behind a meatball.
