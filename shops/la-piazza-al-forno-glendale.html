La Piazza al Forno

Home: Glendale, Arizona
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled, VPN-certified
Signature pie: Meatball pie

La Piazza al Forno works in the Neapolitan tradition in Glendale, AZ, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

That is the bar La Piazza al Forno sets for itself in Glendale, pie after pie. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. That is how La Piazza al Forno keeps it in Glendale.

Here the meatball is crumbled, VPN-certified. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. That is how La Piazza al Forno keeps it in Glendale.
