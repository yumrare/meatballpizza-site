Slice Pizza & Brew

Home: Birmingham, Alabama
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled, Lakeview
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Slice Pizza & Brew works in the Wood-fired tradition in Birmingham, AL, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

It is the standard Slice Pizza & Brew was built on in Birmingham, and the one its regulars enforce. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. Birmingham has other pies; Slice Pizza & Brew stakes its name on this one.

Here the meatball is crumbled, Lakeview. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. Slice Pizza & Brew keeps that standard in Birmingham, night after night.

Meatball pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. In Birmingham, Slice Pizza & Brew would not serve it any other way.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. Birmingham has other pies; Slice Pizza & Brew stakes its name on this one.

Slice Pizza & Brew earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
