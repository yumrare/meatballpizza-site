Montpelier, Vermont · Directory Entry

Positive Pie

Home: Montpelier, Vermont
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Ask regulars in Montpelier where the meatball pizza matters and Positive Pie comes up — a Wood-fired shop whose signature, Meatball pie, earned this page.

Regulars in Montpelier hold Positive Pie to exactly that standard. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. It is the Positive Pie way in Montpelier, and the regulars hold them to it.

The preparation is crumbled — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. In Montpelier, Positive Pie would not serve it any other way.

Order Meatball pie — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. Positive Pie keeps that standard in Montpelier, night after night.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. Montpelier has other pies; Positive Pie stakes its name on this one.

Ask around Montpelier and Positive Pie regulars will tell you the same. Why does the meatball pie matter enough to anchor a national record? Because it grades everything at once. The grind shows in the texture. The seasoning shows in the first bite. The breadcrumb and the milk — or their absence — show in the tenderness. A kitchen can hide behind a fashionable crust or a rare cheese; it cannot hide behind a meatball.

That is the whole of the Positive Pie entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
