Guido's Pizzeria

Home: St. Louis, Missouri
Style: St. Louis style
The meatball: crumbled on The Hill
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Guido's Pizzeria works in the St. Louis style tradition in St. Louis, MO, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

At Guido's Pizzeria in St. Louis, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. St. Louis style rides on Provel — a processed blend of cheddar, Swiss, and provolone that melts like no other cheese — over a cracker crust, cut in squares and rectangles. In St. Louis, Guido's Pizzeria would not serve it any other way.

The meatball itself comes crumbled on The Hill: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. St. Louis has other pies; Guido's Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.

The signature is Meatball pie. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. It is the Guido's Pizzeria way in St. Louis, and the regulars hold them to it.

Shops like this one hold a neighborhood together: the birthday pie, the Friday pie, the pie after the game. The meatball version is the connoisseur's standing order among them. St. Louis has other pies; Guido's Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.
