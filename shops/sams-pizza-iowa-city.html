Sam's Pizza

Home: Iowa City, Iowa
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

Ask regulars in Iowa City where the meatball pizza matters and Sam's Pizza comes up — a Chicago tavern thin shop whose signature, Meatball tavern pie, earned this page.

It is the standard Sam's Pizza was built on in Iowa City, and the one its regulars enforce. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. Iowa City has other pies; Sam's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. In Iowa City, Sam's Pizza would not serve it any other way.

Order Meatball tavern pie — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. It is the Sam's Pizza way in Iowa City, and the regulars hold them to it.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. That is how Sam's Pizza keeps it in Iowa City.

That is the whole of the Sam's Pizza entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
