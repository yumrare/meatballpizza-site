Five Points Pizza

Home: Nashville, Tennessee
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, East Nashville
Signature pie: Meatball slice

In Nashville, TN, the meatball pizza at Five Points Pizza is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a New York slice pie judged by its hardest topping.

Five Points Pizza in Nashville is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Nashville has other pies; Five Points Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The meatball itself comes sliced, East Nashville: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. In Nashville, Five Points Pizza would not serve it any other way.
