Pizzeria Limone

Home: Salt Lake City, Utah
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled with lemon fame
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Pizzeria Limone (Salt Lake City, UT) keeps the Neapolitan faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

That is the bar Pizzeria Limone sets for itself in Salt Lake City, pie after pie. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. It is the Pizzeria Limone way in Salt Lake City, and the regulars hold them to it.

The meatball itself comes crumbled with lemon fame: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. That is how Pizzeria Limone keeps it in Salt Lake City.
