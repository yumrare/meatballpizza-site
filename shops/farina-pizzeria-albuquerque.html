Farina Pizzeria

Home: Albuquerque, New Mexico
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled downtown
Signature pie: Meatball pie

A meatball forgives nothing, and Farina Pizzeria in Albuquerque, NM builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a Neapolitan crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

Farina Pizzeria in Albuquerque is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. Albuquerque has other pies; Farina Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.

Here the meatball is crumbled downtown. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. In Albuquerque, Farina Pizzeria would not serve it any other way.

Order Meatball pie — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. That is how Farina Pizzeria keeps it in Albuquerque.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. It is the Farina Pizzeria way in Albuquerque, and the regulars hold them to it.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. Farina Pizzeria is filed under Albuquerque, NM, in the Neapolitan chapter.
