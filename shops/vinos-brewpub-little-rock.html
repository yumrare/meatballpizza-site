Vino's Brewpub

Home: Little Rock, Arkansas
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

In Little Rock, AR, the meatball pizza at Vino's Brewpub is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a New York slice pie judged by its hardest topping.

That is the bar Vino's Brewpub sets for itself in Little Rock, pie after pie. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Vino's Brewpub keeps that standard in Little Rock, night after night.

The meatball itself comes sliced: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. In Little Rock, Vino's Brewpub would not serve it any other way.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. It is the Vino's Brewpub way in Little Rock, and the regulars hold them to it.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. Vino's Brewpub keeps that standard in Little Rock, night after night.
