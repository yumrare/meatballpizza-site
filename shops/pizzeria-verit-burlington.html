Pizzeria Verità

Home: Burlington, Vermont
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled, Vermont Neapolitan
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Pizzeria Verità is Burlington's entry in the meatball pizza record — a Neapolitan kitchen in VT where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

Regulars in Burlington hold Pizzeria Verità to exactly that standard. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. In Burlington, Pizzeria Verità would not serve it any other way.

The preparation is crumbled, Vermont Neapolitan — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. Burlington has other pies; Pizzeria Verità stakes its name on this one.

Order Meatball pie — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. Burlington has other pies; Pizzeria Verità stakes its name on this one.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. That is how Pizzeria Verità keeps it in Burlington.

Pizzeria Verità earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
