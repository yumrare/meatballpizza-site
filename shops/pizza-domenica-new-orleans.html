Pizza Domenica

Home: New Orleans, Louisiana
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled, Magazine Street
Signature pie: Meatball pie

A meatball forgives nothing, and Pizza Domenica in New Orleans, LA builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a Wood-fired crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

Regulars in New Orleans hold Pizza Domenica to exactly that standard. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. New Orleans has other pies; Pizza Domenica stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is crumbled, Magazine Street — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. Pizza Domenica keeps that standard in New Orleans, night after night.

Meatball pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. In New Orleans, Pizza Domenica would not serve it any other way.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. That is how Pizza Domenica keeps it in New Orleans.

That is the whole of the Pizza Domenica entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
