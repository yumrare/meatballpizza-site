Goodfella's

Home: Staten Island, New York
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, vodka sauce base
Signature pie: Meatball vodka slice

A meatball forgives nothing, and Goodfella's in Staten Island, NY builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a New York slice crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

That is the bar Goodfella's sets for itself in Staten Island, pie after pie. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Staten Island has other pies; Goodfella's stakes its name on this one.

The meatball itself comes sliced, vodka sauce base: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. It is the Goodfella's way in Staten Island, and the regulars hold them to it.

Order Meatball vodka slice — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. That is how Goodfella's keeps it in Staten Island.

Shops like this one hold a neighborhood together: the birthday pie, the Friday pie, the pie after the game. The meatball version is the connoisseur's standing order among them. In Staten Island, Goodfella's would not serve it any other way.

That is the whole of the Goodfella's entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
