Tortugas Pizza

Home: Birmingham, Alabama
Style: Chicago deep dish
The meatball: crumbled, Southside deep dish
Signature pie: Meatball deep dish

Tortugas Pizza works in the Chicago deep dish tradition in Birmingham, AL, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

Tortugas Pizza works to that standard in Birmingham — and asks to be judged by it. Deep dish is a construction, not a crust with ambitions: a buttery pan walls in layers of cheese and filling under chunky tomato, and it is eaten with a knife and fork without apology. Tortugas Pizza keeps that standard in Birmingham, night after night.

The meatball itself comes crumbled, Southside deep dish: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. Tortugas Pizza keeps that standard in Birmingham, night after night.

The pie to know is Meatball deep dish. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. Tortugas Pizza keeps that standard in Birmingham, night after night.

Tortugas Pizza earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
