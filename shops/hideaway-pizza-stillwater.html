Stillwater, Oklahoma · Landmark Oven

Hideaway Pizza

Home: Stillwater, Oklahoma
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced thin on a blistered crust
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Hideaway Pizza (Stillwater, OK) keeps the New York slice faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

It is the standard Hideaway Pizza was built on in Stillwater, and the one its regulars enforce. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. In Stillwater, Hideaway Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The meatball itself comes sliced thin on a blistered crust: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. Hideaway Pizza keeps that standard in Stillwater, night after night.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Hideaway Pizza keeps that standard in Stillwater, night after night.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. In Stillwater, Hideaway Pizza would not serve it any other way.

Ask around Stillwater and Hideaway Pizza regulars will tell you the same. Why does the meatball pie matter enough to anchor a national record? Because it grades everything at once. The grind shows in the texture. The seasoning shows in the first bite. The breadcrumb and the milk — or their absence — show in the tenderness. A kitchen can hide behind a fashionable crust or a rare cheese; it cannot hide behind a meatball.

On OK's pizza map, Stillwater is not a pass-through — it is a pie town, and rooms like Hideaway Pizza are why. The New York slice chapter of this record is thick in this part of the country, and the meatball pie is its most argued-over page.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. Hideaway Pizza is filed under Stillwater, OK, in the New York slice chapter.
