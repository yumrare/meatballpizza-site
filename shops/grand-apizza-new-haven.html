New Haven, Connecticut · Directory Entry

Grand Apizza

Home: New Haven, Connecticut
Style: New Haven apizza
The meatball: crumbled, Grand Avenue style
Signature pie: Meatball apizza

The directory files Grand Apizza of New Haven, CT under New Haven apizza — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball apizza.

Grand Apizza works to that standard in New Haven — and asks to be judged by it. Apizza — New Haven's dialect is New Haven's dialect — coal-fired, charred at the edges, tomato-forward, with pecorino's salt over mozzarella's pull. It is pronounced 'abeetz' and defended like a hometown team. Grand Apizza keeps that standard in New Haven, night after night.

The preparation is crumbled, Grand Avenue style — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. That is how Grand Apizza keeps it in New Haven.

Meatball apizza is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. In New Haven, Grand Apizza would not serve it any other way.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. New Haven has other pies; Grand Apizza stakes its name on this one.

That is the whole of the Grand Apizza entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
