The Bake Shop

Home: Girdwood, Alaska
Style: Sourdough slice
The meatball: crumbled over sourdough crust
Signature pie: Meatball slice

The Bake Shop works in the Sourdough slice tradition in Girdwood, AK, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

Regulars in Girdwood hold The Bake Shop to exactly that standard. A sourdough crust brings tang and chew to the slice format — the bakery's loaf, reimagined as a pie. It is the The Bake Shop way in Girdwood, and the regulars hold them to it.

Here the meatball is crumbled over sourdough crust. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. That is how The Bake Shop keeps it in Girdwood.

The pie to know is Meatball slice. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. Girdwood has other pies; The Bake Shop stakes its name on this one.
