Lamp Woodfired Pizzeria

Home: Scottsdale, Arizona
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Lamp Woodfired Pizzeria (Scottsdale, AZ) keeps the Wood-fired faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

It is the standard Lamp Woodfired Pizzeria was built on in Scottsdale, and the one its regulars enforce. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. Lamp Woodfired Pizzeria keeps that standard in Scottsdale, night after night.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. Lamp Woodfired Pizzeria keeps that standard in Scottsdale, night after night.

The pie to know is Meatball pie. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. That is how Lamp Woodfired Pizzeria keeps it in Scottsdale.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. Scottsdale has other pies; Lamp Woodfired Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.

That is the whole of the Lamp Woodfired Pizzeria entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
