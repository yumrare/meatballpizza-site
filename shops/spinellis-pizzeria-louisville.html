Spinelli's Pizzeria

Home: Louisville, Kentucky
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, Philly attitude
Signature pie: Meatball slice

The directory files Spinelli's Pizzeria of Louisville, KY under New York slice — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball slice.

It is the standard Spinelli's Pizzeria was built on in Louisville, and the one its regulars enforce. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Louisville has other pies; Spinelli's Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.

Here the meatball is sliced, Philly attitude — sliced before it meets the pie, which is the pizzeria's oldest trick for even cooking: no raw centers, no rolling cargo, just meat that crisps where the oven can reach it. It is the Spinelli's Pizzeria way in Louisville, and the regulars hold them to it.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. In Louisville, Spinelli's Pizzeria would not serve it any other way.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. In Louisville, Spinelli's Pizzeria would not serve it any other way.

That is the whole of the Spinelli's Pizzeria entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
