Pizza Rock

Home: Las Vegas, Nevada
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled, Tony Gemignani's Vegas room
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Ask regulars in Las Vegas where the meatball pizza matters and Pizza Rock comes up — a Neapolitan shop whose signature, Meatball pie, earned this page.

Regulars in Las Vegas hold Pizza Rock to exactly that standard. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. It is the Pizza Rock way in Las Vegas, and the regulars hold them to it.

Here the meatball is crumbled, Tony Gemignani's Vegas room. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests.
