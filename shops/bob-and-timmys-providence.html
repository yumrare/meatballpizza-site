Bob & Timmy's

Home: Providence, Rhode Island
Style: Grilled pizza
The meatball: crumbled over grilled crust
Signature pie: Grilled meatball pizza

Bob & Timmy's (Providence, RI) keeps the Grilled pizza faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

At Bob & Timmy's in Providence, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. Grilled pizza cooks the dough directly over fire, flipped once, topped lightly — the technique Al Forno invented in Providence and the country adopted. Providence has other pies; Bob & Timmy's stakes its name on this one.

The meatball itself comes crumbled over grilled crust: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. Providence has other pies; Bob & Timmy's stakes its name on this one.

The pie to know is Grilled meatball pizza. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. Bob & Timmy's keeps that standard in Providence, night after night.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. Providence has other pies; Bob & Timmy's stakes its name on this one.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. Bob & Timmy's is filed under Providence, RI, in the Grilled pizza chapter.
