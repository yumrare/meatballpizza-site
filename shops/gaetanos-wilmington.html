Gaetano's

Home: Wilmington, Delaware
Style: Tomato pie
The meatball: crumbled over Delaware tomato pie
Signature pie: Meatball tomato pie

In Wilmington, DE, the meatball pizza at Gaetano's is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a Tomato pie pie judged by its hardest topping.

Gaetano's in Wilmington is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The tomato pie inverts the usual order — a light hand with cheese under a bright, chunky tomato layer — a Trenton and South Philadelphia creed. Wilmington has other pies; Gaetano's stakes its name on this one.

Here the meatball is crumbled over Delaware tomato pie. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. That is how Gaetano's keeps it in Wilmington.
