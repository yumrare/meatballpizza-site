Menomale

Home: Washington, District of Columbia
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled, Brookland oven
Signature pie: Meatball pie

A meatball forgives nothing, and Menomale in Washington, DC builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a Neapolitan crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

At Menomale in Washington, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. That is how Menomale keeps it in Washington.

Here the meatball is crumbled, Brookland oven. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. In Washington, Menomale would not serve it any other way.

The pie to know is Meatball pie. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. Washington has other pies; Menomale stakes its name on this one.
