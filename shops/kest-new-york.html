Kesté

Home: New York, New York
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled, wood-oven kissed
Signature pie: Meatball montanara style

The directory files Kesté of New York, NY under Neapolitan — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball montanara style.

Regulars in New York hold Kesté to exactly that standard. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. Kesté keeps that standard in New York, night after night.

Here the meatball is crumbled, wood-oven kissed. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. New York has other pies; Kesté stakes its name on this one.

Order Meatball montanara style — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. It is the Kesté way in New York, and the regulars hold them to it.

Shops like this one hold a neighborhood together: the birthday pie, the Friday pie, the pie after the game. The meatball version is the connoisseur's standing order among them. That is how Kesté keeps it in New York.

Kesté earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
