Isola Pizza Bar

Home: San Diego, California
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

The directory files Isola Pizza Bar of San Diego, CA under Neapolitan — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball pie.

Isola Pizza Bar works to that standard in San Diego — and asks to be judged by it. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. In San Diego, Isola Pizza Bar would not serve it any other way.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. It is the Isola Pizza Bar way in San Diego, and the regulars hold them to it.

Meatball pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. Isola Pizza Bar keeps that standard in San Diego, night after night.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. That is how Isola Pizza Bar keeps it in San Diego.

Isola Pizza Bar earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
