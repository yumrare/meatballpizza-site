Lucy's New York Style Pizzeria

Home: Idaho Falls, Idaho
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

A meatball forgives nothing, and Lucy's New York Style Pizzeria in Idaho Falls, ID builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a New York slice crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

Lucy's New York Style Pizzeria in Idaho Falls is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Idaho Falls has other pies; Lucy's New York Style Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.

The meatball itself comes sliced: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. It is the Lucy's New York Style Pizzeria way in Idaho Falls, and the regulars hold them to it.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. It is the Lucy's New York Style Pizzeria way in Idaho Falls, and the regulars hold them to it.
