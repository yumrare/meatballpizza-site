Zelda's

Home: Sacramento, California
Style: Chicago deep dish
The meatball: crumbled, house style
Signature pie: Meatball deep dish

Zelda's (Sacramento, CA) keeps the Chicago deep dish faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

Regulars in Sacramento hold Zelda's to exactly that standard. Deep dish is a construction, not a crust with ambitions: a buttery pan walls in layers of cheese and filling under chunky tomato, and it is eaten with a knife and fork without apology. Sacramento has other pies; Zelda's stakes its name on this one.

The meatball itself comes crumbled, house style: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. It is the Zelda's way in Sacramento, and the regulars hold them to it.

Meatball deep dish is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. Sacramento has other pies; Zelda's stakes its name on this one.

Shops like this one hold a neighborhood together: the birthday pie, the Friday pie, the pie after the game. The meatball version is the connoisseur's standing order among them. It is the Zelda's way in Sacramento, and the regulars hold them to it.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. Zelda's is filed under Sacramento, CA, in the Chicago deep dish chapter.
