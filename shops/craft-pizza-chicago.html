Craft Pizza

Home: Chicago, Illinois
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, Wicker Park
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Craft Pizza works in the New York slice tradition in Chicago, IL, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

It is the standard Craft Pizza was built on in Chicago, and the one its regulars enforce. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. In Chicago, Craft Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The meatball itself comes sliced, Wicker Park: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. It is the Craft Pizza way in Chicago, and the regulars hold them to it.
