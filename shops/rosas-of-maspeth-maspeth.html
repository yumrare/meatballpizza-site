Rosa's of Maspeth

Home: Maspeth, New York
Style: Sicilian
The meatball: crumbled over squares
Signature pie: Meatball square

Ask regulars in Maspeth where the meatball pizza matters and Rosa's of Maspeth comes up — a Sicilian shop whose signature, Meatball square, earned this page.

Rosa's of Maspeth in Maspeth is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The Sicilian square is a bakery pie: a thick, olive-oil-crisped crust, sauce over or under the cheese depending on the house, and corners that regulars request by name. That is how Rosa's of Maspeth keeps it in Maspeth.

Here the meatball is crumbled over squares. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. Rosa's of Maspeth keeps that standard in Maspeth, night after night.

The signature is Meatball square. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Maspeth has other pies; Rosa's of Maspeth stakes its name on this one.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. Maspeth has other pies; Rosa's of Maspeth stakes its name on this one.
