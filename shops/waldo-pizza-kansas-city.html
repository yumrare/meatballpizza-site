Waldo Pizza

Home: Kansas City, Missouri
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled, house style
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

Ask regulars in Kansas City where the meatball pizza matters and Waldo Pizza comes up — a Chicago tavern thin shop whose signature, Meatball tavern pie, earned this page.

At Waldo Pizza in Kansas City, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. Kansas City has other pies; Waldo Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is crumbled, house style — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. In Kansas City, Waldo Pizza would not serve it any other way.
