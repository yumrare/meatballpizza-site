Police Station Pizza

Home: Ambridge, Pennsylvania
Style: Ohio Valley style
The meatball: crumbled under cold cheese
Signature pie: Meatball square

In Ambridge, PA, the meatball pizza at Police Station Pizza is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a Ohio Valley style pie judged by its hardest topping.

That is the bar Police Station Pizza sets for itself in Ambridge, pie after pie. Ohio Valley style bakes the crust and sauce first, then buries the hot pie under cold provolone and toppings after the oven — a bite that is hot and cold at once. That is how Police Station Pizza keeps it in Ambridge.
