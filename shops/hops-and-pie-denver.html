Hops & Pie

Home: Denver, Colorado
Style: Detroit style
The meatball: crumbled on Detroit squares
Signature pie: Meatball square

Hops & Pie works in the Detroit style tradition in Denver, CO, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

Hops & Pie works to that standard in Denver — and asks to be judged by it. Detroit style bakes in blue-steel pans to a frico edge — cheese crisped against the metal all the way around — with toppings carried to the very corner. It is the Hops & Pie way in Denver, and the regulars hold them to it.

The meatball itself comes crumbled on Detroit squares: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. In Denver, Hops & Pie would not serve it any other way.

Meatball square is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. Hops & Pie keeps that standard in Denver, night after night.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. It is the Hops & Pie way in Denver, and the regulars hold them to it.

That is the whole of the Hops & Pie entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
