Bruno's Pizza

Home: South Bend, Indiana
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced thin on a blistered crust
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Bruno's Pizza is South Bend's entry in the meatball pizza record — a New York slice kitchen in IN where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

Bruno's Pizza works to that standard in South Bend — and asks to be judged by it. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. South Bend has other pies; Bruno's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is sliced thin on a blistered crust. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. Bruno's Pizza keeps that standard in South Bend, night after night.

Meatball slice is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. That is how Bruno's Pizza keeps it in South Bend.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. Bruno's Pizza keeps that standard in South Bend, night after night.

That is the whole of the Bruno's Pizza entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
