Hounddog's Pizza

Home: Columbus, Ohio
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, campus institution
Signature pie: Meatball slice

A meatball forgives nothing, and Hounddog's Pizza in Columbus, OH builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a New York slice crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

That is the bar Hounddog's Pizza sets for itself in Columbus, pie after pie. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. It is the Hounddog's Pizza way in Columbus, and the regulars hold them to it.

The meatball itself comes sliced, campus institution: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. It is the Hounddog's Pizza way in Columbus, and the regulars hold them to it.

Meatball slice is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. In Columbus, Hounddog's Pizza would not serve it any other way.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. Hounddog's Pizza keeps that standard in Columbus, night after night.

Hounddog's Pizza is filed in the New York slice chapter, under Columbus, OH. Menus change; the record keeps the signature as it stands.
