The Wylder

Home: Boise, Idaho
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled, downtown Boise
Signature pie: Meatball pie

The record of Wood-fired pizza in ID runs through rooms like The Wylder in Boise, where the meatball pie is treated as the main event rather than an option.

It is the standard The Wylder was built on in Boise, and the one its regulars enforce. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. That is how The Wylder keeps it in Boise.

The preparation is crumbled, downtown Boise — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. Boise has other pies; The Wylder stakes its name on this one.
