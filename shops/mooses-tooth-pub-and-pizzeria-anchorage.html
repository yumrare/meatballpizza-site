Moose's Tooth Pub & Pizzeria

Home: Anchorage, Alaska
Style: California artisan
The meatball: crumbled with apricot sauce fame nearby
Signature pie: Meatball pie, Anchorage style
On the record since: 1996

Moose's Tooth Pub & Pizzeria is Anchorage's entry in the meatball pizza record — a California artisan kitchen in AK where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

Regulars in Anchorage hold Moose's Tooth Pub & Pizzeria to exactly that standard. Moose's Tooth Pub & Pizzeria keeps that standard in Anchorage, night after night.

Ask around Anchorage and Moose's Tooth Pub & Pizzeria regulars will tell you the same. Why does the meatball pie matter enough to anchor a national record? Because it grades everything at once. The grind shows in the texture. The seasoning shows in the first bite. The breadcrumb and the milk — or their absence — show in the tenderness. A kitchen can hide behind a fashionable crust or a rare cheese; it cannot hide behind a meatball.

On AK's pizza map, Anchorage is not a pass-through — it is a pie town, and rooms like Moose's Tooth Pub & Pizzeria are why. The California artisan chapter of this record is thick in this part of the country, and the meatball pie is its most argued-over page.

At Moose's Tooth Pub & Pizzeria in Anchorage, the point deserves underlining. The meatball pizza also carries a history the fancier pies do not: it is the Italian-American kitchen meeting the pizzeria head-on — Sunday sauce meat, baked in a ball, meeting the coal oven's crust. When a shop serves it proudly, it is serving its own family's cooking in public.
