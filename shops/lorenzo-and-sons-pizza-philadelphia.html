Philadelphia, Pennsylvania · Directory Entry

Lorenzo and Sons Pizza

Home: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, jumbo South Street slices
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Ask regulars in Philadelphia where the meatball pizza matters and Lorenzo and Sons Pizza comes up — a New York slice shop whose signature, Meatball slice, earned this page.

That is the bar Lorenzo and Sons Pizza sets for itself in Philadelphia, pie after pie. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. It is the Lorenzo and Sons Pizza way in Philadelphia, and the regulars hold them to it.

Here the meatball is sliced, jumbo South Street slices — sliced before it meets the pie, which is the pizzeria's oldest trick for even cooking: no raw centers, no rolling cargo, just meat that crisps where the oven can reach it. In Philadelphia, Lorenzo and Sons Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Philadelphia has other pies; Lorenzo and Sons Pizza stakes its name on this one.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. In Philadelphia, Lorenzo and Sons Pizza would not serve it any other way.

That is the whole of the Lorenzo and Sons Pizza entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
