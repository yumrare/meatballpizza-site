Metro Pizza

Home: Las Vegas, Nevada
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced thin on a blistered crust
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Ask regulars in Las Vegas where the meatball pizza matters and Metro Pizza comes up — a New York slice shop whose signature, Meatball slice, earned this page.

Regulars in Las Vegas hold Metro Pizza to exactly that standard. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Las Vegas has other pies; Metro Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is sliced thin on a blistered crust. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. In Las Vegas, Metro Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The pie to know is Meatball slice. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. In Las Vegas, Metro Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. Metro Pizza keeps that standard in Las Vegas, night after night.

Ask around Las Vegas and Metro Pizza regulars will tell you the same. Why does the meatball pie matter enough to anchor a national record? Because it grades everything at once. The grind shows in the texture. The seasoning shows in the first bite. The breadcrumb and the milk — or their absence — show in the tenderness. A kitchen can hide behind a fashionable crust or a rare cheese; it cannot hide behind a meatball.

On NV's pizza map, Las Vegas is not a pass-through — it is a pie town, and rooms like Metro Pizza are why. The New York slice chapter of this record is thick in this part of the country, and the meatball pie is its most argued-over page.

There is a reason Metro Pizza draws its Las Vegas following. The meatball pizza also carries a history the fancier pies do not: it is the Italian-American kitchen meeting the pizzeria head-on — Sunday sauce meat, baked in a ball, meeting the coal oven's crust. When a shop serves it proudly, it is serving its own family's cooking in public.

Metro Pizza earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
