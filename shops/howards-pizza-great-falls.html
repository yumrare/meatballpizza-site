Howard's Pizza

Home: Great Falls, Montana
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled, house style
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

Howard's Pizza works in the Chicago tavern thin tradition in Great Falls, MT, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

That is the bar Howard's Pizza sets for itself in Great Falls, pie after pie. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. In Great Falls, Howard's Pizza would not serve it any other way.
