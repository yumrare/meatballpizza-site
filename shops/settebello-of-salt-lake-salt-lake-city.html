Settebello of Salt Lake

Home: Salt Lake City, Utah
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Settebello of Salt Lake (Salt Lake City, UT) keeps the Neapolitan faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

Settebello of Salt Lake works to that standard in Salt Lake City — and asks to be judged by it. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. Salt Lake City has other pies; Settebello of Salt Lake stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is crumbled — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. Settebello of Salt Lake keeps that standard in Salt Lake City, night after night.

Order Meatball pie — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. In Salt Lake City, Settebello of Salt Lake would not serve it any other way.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. That is how Settebello of Salt Lake keeps it in Salt Lake City.

That is the whole of the Settebello of Salt Lake entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
