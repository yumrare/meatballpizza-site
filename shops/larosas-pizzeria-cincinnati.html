LaRosa's Pizzeria

Home: Cincinnati, Ohio
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

A meatball forgives nothing, and LaRosa's Pizzeria in Cincinnati, OH builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a New York slice crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

That is the bar LaRosa's Pizzeria sets for itself in Cincinnati, pie after pie. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. That is how LaRosa's Pizzeria keeps it in Cincinnati.

Here the meatball is sliced — sliced before it meets the pie, which is the pizzeria's oldest trick for even cooking: no raw centers, no rolling cargo, just meat that crisps where the oven can reach it. It is the LaRosa's Pizzeria way in Cincinnati, and the regulars hold them to it.

Order Meatball slice — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. That is how LaRosa's Pizzeria keeps it in Cincinnati.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. LaRosa's Pizzeria keeps that standard in Cincinnati, night after night.
