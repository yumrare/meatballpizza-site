Cottage Grove, Wisconsin · Directory Entry

Papa Jimmy's

Home: Cottage Grove, Wisconsin
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

A meatball forgives nothing, and Papa Jimmy's in Cottage Grove, WI builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a Chicago tavern thin crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

Papa Jimmy's works to that standard in Cottage Grove — and asks to be judged by it. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. Papa Jimmy's keeps that standard in Cottage Grove, night after night.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. Cottage Grove has other pies; Papa Jimmy's stakes its name on this one.

The signature is Meatball tavern pie. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. In Cottage Grove, Papa Jimmy's would not serve it any other way.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. That is how Papa Jimmy's keeps it in Cottage Grove.

That is the whole of the Papa Jimmy's entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
