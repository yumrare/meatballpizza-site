Tutta Bella Neapolitan Pizzeria

Home: Seattle, Washington
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled, first VPN in the Northwest
Signature pie: Meatball pie

A meatball forgives nothing, and Tutta Bella Neapolitan Pizzeria in Seattle, WA builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a Neapolitan crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

Regulars in Seattle hold Tutta Bella Neapolitan Pizzeria to exactly that standard. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. Tutta Bella Neapolitan Pizzeria keeps that standard in Seattle, night after night.

The meatball itself comes crumbled, first VPN in the Northwest: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. That is how Tutta Bella Neapolitan Pizzeria keeps it in Seattle.

Meatball pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. In Seattle, Tutta Bella Neapolitan Pizzeria would not serve it any other way.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. Seattle has other pies; Tutta Bella Neapolitan Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.

Tutta Bella Neapolitan Pizzeria earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
