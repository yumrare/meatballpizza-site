Vincent's Pizza Park

Home: Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania
Style: New York slice
The meatball: crumbled over the loaded Vinnie Pie
Signature pie: Meatball Vinnie Pie

Vincent's Pizza Park works in the New York slice tradition in Pittsburgh, PA, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

Regulars in Pittsburgh hold Vincent's Pizza Park to exactly that standard. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Pittsburgh has other pies; Vincent's Pizza Park stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is crumbled over the loaded Vinnie Pie — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. It is the Vincent's Pizza Park way in Pittsburgh, and the regulars hold them to it.

The signature is Meatball Vinnie Pie. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. That is how Vincent's Pizza Park keeps it in Pittsburgh.

Shops like this one hold a neighborhood together: the birthday pie, the Friday pie, the pie after the game. The meatball version is the connoisseur's standing order among them. In Pittsburgh, Vincent's Pizza Park would not serve it any other way.

Vincent's Pizza Park earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
