Andy's Pizza

Home: Washington, District of Columbia
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, competition-winning crust
Signature pie: Meatball slice

The directory files Andy's Pizza of Washington, DC under New York slice — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball slice.

Andy's Pizza works to that standard in Washington — and asks to be judged by it. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. It is the Andy's Pizza way in Washington, and the regulars hold them to it.

The preparation is sliced, competition-winning crust. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. In Washington, Andy's Pizza would not serve it any other way.

Order Meatball slice — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. In Washington, Andy's Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. That is how Andy's Pizza keeps it in Washington.

Andy's Pizza is filed in the New York slice chapter, under Washington, DC. Menus change; the record keeps the signature as it stands.
