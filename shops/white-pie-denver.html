White Pie

Home: Denver, Colorado
Style: New Haven apizza
The meatball: crumbled, Denver apizza
Signature pie: Meatball apizza

Ask regulars in Denver where the meatball pizza matters and White Pie comes up — a New Haven apizza shop whose signature, Meatball apizza, earned this page.

White Pie in Denver is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. Apizza — New Haven's dialect is New Haven's dialect — coal-fired, charred at the edges, tomato-forward, with pecorino's salt over mozzarella's pull. It is pronounced 'abeetz' and defended like a hometown team. Denver has other pies; White Pie stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is crumbled, Denver apizza — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. In Denver, White Pie would not serve it any other way.

The signature is Meatball apizza. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Denver has other pies; White Pie stakes its name on this one.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. That is how White Pie keeps it in Denver.

White Pie earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
