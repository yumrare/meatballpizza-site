Bella Napoli of Belmar

Home: Belmar, New Jersey
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Ask regulars in Belmar where the meatball pizza matters and Bella Napoli of Belmar comes up — a New York slice shop whose signature, Meatball slice, earned this page.

Regulars in Belmar hold Bella Napoli of Belmar to exactly that standard. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Here the meatball is sliced — sliced before it meets the pie, which is the pizzeria's oldest trick for even cooking: no raw centers, no rolling cargo, just meat that crisps where the oven can reach it. Belmar has other pies; Bella Napoli of Belmar stakes its name on this one.
