Maria's Pizza

Home: Milwaukee, Wisconsin
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled, house style
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

Maria's Pizza works in the Chicago tavern thin tradition in Milwaukee, WI, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

Regulars in Milwaukee hold Maria's Pizza to exactly that standard. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. Maria's Pizza keeps that standard in Milwaukee, night after night.

The meatball itself comes crumbled, house style: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. The pie to know is Meatball tavern pie. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. Milwaukee has other pies; Maria's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. It is the Maria's Pizza way in Milwaukee, and the regulars hold them to it.

At Maria's Pizza in Milwaukee, the point deserves underlining. Why does the meatball pie matter enough to anchor a national record? Because it grades everything at once.
