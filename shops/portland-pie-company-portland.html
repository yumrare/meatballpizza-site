Portland Pie Company

Home: Portland, Maine
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced with flavored crusts
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Portland Pie Company is Portland's entry in the meatball pizza record — a New York slice kitchen in ME where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

At Portland Pie Company in Portland, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. It is the Portland Pie Company way in Portland, and the regulars hold them to it.

The preparation is sliced with flavored crusts. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. That is how Portland Pie Company keeps it in Portland.

The pie to know is Meatball slice. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. Portland has other pies; Portland Pie Company stakes its name on this one.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. That is how Portland Pie Company keeps it in Portland.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. Portland Pie Company is filed under Portland, ME, in the New York slice chapter.
