Bruno's Pizzeria

Home: Clifton, New Jersey
Style: Sicilian
The meatball: crumbled over squares
Signature pie: Meatball square

The directory files Bruno's Pizzeria of Clifton, NJ under Sicilian — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball square.

At Bruno's Pizzeria in Clifton, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. The Sicilian square is a bakery pie: a thick, olive-oil-crisped crust, sauce over or under the cheese depending on the house, and corners that regulars request by name. It is the Bruno's Pizzeria way in Clifton, and the regulars hold them to it.

Here the meatball is crumbled over squares. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. It is the Bruno's Pizzeria way in Clifton, and the regulars hold them to it.
