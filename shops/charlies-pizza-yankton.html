Charlie's Pizza

Home: Yankton, South Dakota
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled, house style
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

A meatball forgives nothing, and Charlie's Pizza in Yankton, SD builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a Chicago tavern thin crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

At Charlie's Pizza in Yankton, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. In Yankton, Charlie's Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The meatball itself comes crumbled, house style: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps.
