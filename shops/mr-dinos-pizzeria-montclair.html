Mr. Dino's Pizzeria

Home: Montclair, New Jersey
Style: Sicilian
The meatball: crumbled over squares
Signature pie: Meatball square

A meatball forgives nothing, and Mr. Dino's Pizzeria in Montclair, NJ builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a Sicilian crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

Regulars in Montclair hold Mr. Dino's Pizzeria to exactly that standard. The Sicilian square is a bakery pie: a thick, olive-oil-crisped crust, sauce over or under the cheese depending on the house, and corners that regulars request by name. That is how Mr. Dino's Pizzeria keeps it in Montclair.

The meatball itself comes crumbled over squares: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. Mr. Dino's Pizzeria keeps that standard in Montclair, night after night.
