Big Boys Pizza

Home: Chicago, Illinois
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

In Chicago, IL, the meatball pizza at Big Boys Pizza is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a Chicago tavern thin pie judged by its hardest topping.

It is the standard Big Boys Pizza was built on in Chicago, and the one its regulars enforce. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. That is how Big Boys Pizza keeps it in Chicago.

The preparation is crumbled — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. In Chicago, Big Boys Pizza would not serve it any other way.

Meatball tavern pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. In Chicago, Big Boys Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. Chicago has other pies; Big Boys Pizza stakes its name on this one.
