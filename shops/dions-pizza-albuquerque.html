Dion's Pizza

Home: Albuquerque, New Mexico
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Dion's Pizza is Albuquerque's entry in the meatball pizza record — a New York slice kitchen in NM where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

That is the bar Dion's Pizza sets for itself in Albuquerque, pie after pie. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. That is how Dion's Pizza keeps it in Albuquerque.

The meatball itself comes sliced: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. It is the Dion's Pizza way in Albuquerque, and the regulars hold them to it.

The pie to know is Meatball slice. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. It is the Dion's Pizza way in Albuquerque, and the regulars hold them to it.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. That is how Dion's Pizza keeps it in Albuquerque.
