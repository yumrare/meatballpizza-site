Knolla's Pizza

Home: Wichita, Kansas
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced thin on a blistered crust
Signature pie: Meatball slice

A meatball forgives nothing, and Knolla's Pizza in Wichita, KS builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a New York slice crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

Knolla's Pizza in Wichita is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Knolla's Pizza keeps that standard in Wichita, night after night.

The meatball itself comes sliced thin on a blistered crust: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. Wichita has other pies; Knolla's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The pie to know is Meatball slice. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. In Wichita, Knolla's Pizza would not serve it any other way.

Shops like this one hold a neighborhood together: the birthday pie, the Friday pie, the pie after the game. The meatball version is the connoisseur's standing order among them. Knolla's Pizza keeps that standard in Wichita, night after night.
