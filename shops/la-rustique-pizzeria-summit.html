La Rustique Pizzeria

Home: Summit, New Jersey
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

The record of Wood-fired pizza in NJ runs through rooms like La Rustique Pizzeria in Summit, where the meatball pie is treated as the main event rather than an option.

At La Rustique Pizzeria in Summit, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. It is the La Rustique Pizzeria way in Summit, and the regulars hold them to it.

Here the meatball is crumbled. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. Summit has other pies; La Rustique Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.
