MacKenzie River Pizza

Home: Bozeman, Montana
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled, Montana-born chain
Signature pie: Meatball pie

The directory files MacKenzie River Pizza of Bozeman, MT under Wood-fired — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball pie.

That is the bar MacKenzie River Pizza sets for itself in Bozeman, pie after pie. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. In Bozeman, MacKenzie River Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The meatball itself comes crumbled, Montana-born chain: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. In Bozeman, MacKenzie River Pizza would not serve it any other way.

Meatball pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. Bozeman has other pies; MacKenzie River Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. In Bozeman, MacKenzie River Pizza would not serve it any other way.

MacKenzie River Pizza earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
