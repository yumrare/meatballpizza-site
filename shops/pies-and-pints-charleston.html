Charleston, West Virginia · Directory Entry

Pies & Pints

Home: Charleston, West Virginia
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled with craft beer
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Pies & Pints (Charleston, WV) keeps the Wood-fired faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

It is the standard Pies & Pints was built on in Charleston, and the one its regulars enforce. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. In Charleston, Pies & Pints would not serve it any other way.

The meatball itself comes crumbled with craft beer: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. It is the Pies & Pints way in Charleston, and the regulars hold them to it.

Order Meatball pie — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. In Charleston, Pies & Pints would not serve it any other way.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. In Charleston, Pies & Pints would not serve it any other way.
