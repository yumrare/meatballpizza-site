Brooklyn Pizza Company

Home: Tucson, Arizona
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced with solar pies
Signature pie: Meatball slice

The record of New York slice pizza in AZ runs through rooms like Brooklyn Pizza Company in Tucson, where the meatball pie is treated as the main event rather than an option.

That is the bar Brooklyn Pizza Company sets for itself in Tucson, pie after pie. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. It is the Brooklyn Pizza Company way in Tucson, and the regulars hold them to it.

Here the meatball is sliced with solar pies — sliced before it meets the pie, which is the pizzeria's oldest trick for even cooking: no raw centers, no rolling cargo, just meat that crisps where the oven can reach it. That is how Brooklyn Pizza Company keeps it in Tucson.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. In Tucson, Brooklyn Pizza Company would not serve it any other way.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. That is how Brooklyn Pizza Company keeps it in Tucson.
