Mancino's

Home: Summit, New Jersey
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

In Summit, NJ, the meatball pizza at Mancino's is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a New York slice pie judged by its hardest topping.

Regulars in Summit hold Mancino's to exactly that standard. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Summit has other pies; Mancino's stakes its name on this one.

Here the meatball is sliced — sliced before it meets the pie, which is the pizzeria's oldest trick for even cooking: no raw centers, no rolling cargo, just meat that crisps where the oven can reach it. Summit has other pies; Mancino's stakes its name on this one.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Mancino's keeps that standard in Summit, night after night.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. It is the Mancino's way in Summit, and the regulars hold them to it.

That is the whole of the Mancino's entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
