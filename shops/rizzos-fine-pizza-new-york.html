Rizzo's Fine Pizza

Home: New York, New York
Style: Sicilian
The meatball: crumbled over thin squares
Signature pie: Meatball square, Astoria

Rizzo's Fine Pizza is New York's entry in the meatball pizza record — a Sicilian kitchen in NY where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

Regulars in New York hold Rizzo's Fine Pizza to exactly that standard. The Sicilian square is a bakery pie: a thick, olive-oil-crisped crust, sauce over or under the cheese depending on the house, and corners that regulars request by name. It is the Rizzo's Fine Pizza way in New York, and the regulars hold them to it.

The preparation is crumbled over thin squares — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. New York has other pies; Rizzo's Fine Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The pie to know is Meatball square, Astoria. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. Rizzo's Fine Pizza keeps that standard in New York, night after night.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. Rizzo's Fine Pizza keeps that standard in New York, night after night.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. Rizzo's Fine Pizza is filed under New York, NY, in the Sicilian chapter.
