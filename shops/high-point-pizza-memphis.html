High Point Pizza

Home: Memphis, Tennessee
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

The directory files High Point Pizza of Memphis, TN under New York slice — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball slice.

That is the bar High Point Pizza sets for itself in Memphis, pie after pie. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. It is the High Point Pizza way in Memphis, and the regulars hold them to it.

The meatball itself comes sliced: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. High Point Pizza keeps that standard in Memphis, night after night.

Meatball slice is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. That is how High Point Pizza keeps it in Memphis.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. In Memphis, High Point Pizza would not serve it any other way.

High Point Pizza earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
