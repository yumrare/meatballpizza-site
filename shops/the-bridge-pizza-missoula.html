The Bridge Pizza

Home: Missoula, Montana
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

The Bridge Pizza works in the New York slice tradition in Missoula, MT, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

Regulars in Missoula hold The Bridge Pizza to exactly that standard. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. That is how The Bridge Pizza keeps it in Missoula.

The meatball itself comes sliced: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. In Missoula, The Bridge Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The pie to know is Meatball slice. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. In Missoula, The Bridge Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. Missoula has other pies; The Bridge Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The Bridge Pizza is filed in the New York slice chapter, under Missoula, MT. Menus change; the record keeps the signature as it stands.
