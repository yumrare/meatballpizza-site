Cloverleaf Restaurant

Home: Eastpointe, Michigan
Style: Detroit style
The meatball: crumbled, Gus Guerra's other original
Signature pie: Meatball square

Cloverleaf Restaurant (Eastpointe, MI) keeps the Detroit style faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

Cloverleaf Restaurant in Eastpointe is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. Detroit style bakes in blue-steel pans to a frico edge — cheese crisped against the metal all the way around — with toppings carried to the very corner. It is the Cloverleaf Restaurant way in Eastpointe, and the regulars hold them to it.

The meatball itself comes crumbled, Gus Guerra's other original: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps.
