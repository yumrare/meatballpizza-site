Seattle, Washington · Directory Entry

Lupo

Home: Seattle, Washington
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled, Fremont sourdough
Signature pie: Meatball pie

The record of Wood-fired pizza in WA runs through rooms like Lupo in Seattle, where the meatball pie is treated as the main event rather than an option.

Lupo works to that standard in Seattle — and asks to be judged by it. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. Seattle has other pies; Lupo stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is crumbled, Fremont sourdough — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. Seattle has other pies; Lupo stakes its name on this one.

The signature is Meatball pie. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Seattle has other pies; Lupo stakes its name on this one.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. In Seattle, Lupo would not serve it any other way.

Ask around Seattle and Lupo regulars will tell you the same. Why does the meatball pie matter enough to anchor a national record? Because it grades everything at once. The grind shows in the texture. The seasoning shows in the first bite. The breadcrumb and the milk — or their absence — show in the tenderness. A kitchen can hide behind a fashionable crust or a rare cheese; it cannot hide behind a meatball.

Lupo is filed in the Wood-fired chapter, under Seattle, WA. Menus change; the record keeps the signature as it stands.
