Naples Pizza

Home: Farmington, Connecticut
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Ask regulars in Farmington where the meatball pizza matters and Naples Pizza comes up — a New York slice shop whose signature, Meatball slice, earned this page.

That is the bar Naples Pizza sets for itself in Farmington, pie after pie. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. It is the Naples Pizza way in Farmington, and the regulars hold them to it.

The preparation is sliced. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. In Farmington, Naples Pizza would not serve it any other way.
