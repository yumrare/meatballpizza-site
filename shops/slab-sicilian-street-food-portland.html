Portland, Maine · Directory Entry

Slab Sicilian Street Food

Home: Portland, Maine
Style: Sicilian
The meatball: crumbled over hand-slabbed squares
Signature pie: Meatball slab

Ask regulars in Portland where the meatball pizza matters and Slab Sicilian Street Food comes up — a Sicilian shop whose signature, Meatball slab, earned this page.

At Slab Sicilian Street Food in Portland, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. The Sicilian square is a bakery pie: a thick, olive-oil-crisped crust, sauce over or under the cheese depending on the house, and corners that regulars request by name. In Portland, Slab Sicilian Street Food would not serve it any other way.

The meatball itself comes crumbled over hand-slabbed squares: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. It is the Slab Sicilian Street Food way in Portland, and the regulars hold them to it.

The signature is Meatball slab. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Portland has other pies; Slab Sicilian Street Food stakes its name on this one.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. That is how Slab Sicilian Street Food keeps it in Portland.

That is the whole of the Slab Sicilian Street Food entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
