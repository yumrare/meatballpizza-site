In Omaha, NE, the meatball pizza at La Casa Pizzeria is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a New York slice pie judged by its hardest topping.

It is the standard La Casa Pizzeria was built on in Omaha, and the one its regulars enforce. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide.
