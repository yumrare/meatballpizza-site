Portland, Oregon · Directory Entry

Red Sauce Pizza

Home: Portland, Oregon
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

The directory files Red Sauce Pizza of Portland, OR under New York slice — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball slice.

Red Sauce Pizza in Portland is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. It is the Red Sauce Pizza way in Portland, and the regulars hold them to it.

Here the meatball is sliced — sliced before it meets the pie, which is the pizzeria's oldest trick for even cooking: no raw centers, no rolling cargo, just meat that crisps where the oven can reach it. Red Sauce Pizza keeps that standard in Portland, night after night.

The pie to know is Meatball slice. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. In Portland, Red Sauce Pizza would not serve it any other way.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. Portland has other pies; Red Sauce Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. Red Sauce Pizza is filed under Portland, OR, in the New York slice chapter.
