My Friend Derek's

Home: Seattle, Washington
Style: Detroit style
The meatball: crumbled on Detroit squares
Signature pie: Meatball square

Ask regulars in Seattle where the meatball pizza matters and My Friend Derek's comes up — a Detroit style shop whose signature, Meatball square, earned this page.

That is the bar My Friend Derek's sets for itself in Seattle, pie after pie. Detroit style bakes in blue-steel pans to a frico edge — cheese crisped against the metal all the way around — with toppings carried to the very corner. In Seattle, My Friend Derek's would not serve it any other way.

The preparation is crumbled on Detroit squares — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. That is how My Friend Derek's keeps it in Seattle.

Order Meatball square — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. That is how My Friend Derek's keeps it in Seattle.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. My Friend Derek's keeps that standard in Seattle, night after night.
