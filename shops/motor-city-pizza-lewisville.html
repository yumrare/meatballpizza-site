Motor City Pizza

Home: Lewisville, Texas
Style: Detroit style
The meatball: crumbled on Detroit squares
Signature pie: Meatball square

Ask regulars in Lewisville where the meatball pizza matters and Motor City Pizza comes up — a Detroit style shop whose signature, Meatball square, earned this page.

That is the bar Motor City Pizza sets for itself in Lewisville, pie after pie. Detroit style bakes in blue-steel pans to a frico edge — cheese crisped against the metal all the way around — with toppings carried to the very corner. That is how Motor City Pizza keeps it in Lewisville.

The preparation is crumbled on Detroit squares — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. In Lewisville, Motor City Pizza would not serve it any other way.

Meatball square is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. Lewisville has other pies; Motor City Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. Lewisville has other pies; Motor City Pizza stakes its name on this one.

That is the whole of the Motor City Pizza entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
