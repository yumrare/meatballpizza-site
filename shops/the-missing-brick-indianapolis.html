The Missing Brick

Home: Indianapolis, Indiana
Style: Detroit style
The meatball: crumbled on brick squares
Signature pie: Meatball square

The record of Detroit style pizza in IN runs through rooms like The Missing Brick in Indianapolis, where the meatball pie is treated as the main event rather than an option.

It is the standard The Missing Brick was built on in Indianapolis, and the one its regulars enforce. Detroit style bakes in blue-steel pans to a frico edge — cheese crisped against the metal all the way around — with toppings carried to the very corner. That is how The Missing Brick keeps it in Indianapolis.

The meatball itself comes crumbled on brick squares: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. It is the The Missing Brick way in Indianapolis, and the regulars hold them to it.

Meatball square is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. Indianapolis has other pies; The Missing Brick stakes its name on this one.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. It is the The Missing Brick way in Indianapolis, and the regulars hold them to it.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. The Missing Brick is filed under Indianapolis, IN, in the Detroit style chapter.
