Rehoboth, Delaware · Directory Entry

The Crust and Craft

Home: Rehoboth, Delaware
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

The directory files The Crust and Craft of Rehoboth, DE under Wood-fired — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball pie.

At The Crust and Craft in Rehoboth, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. The Crust and Craft keeps that standard in Rehoboth, night after night.

Here the meatball is crumbled. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. It is the The Crust and Craft way in Rehoboth, and the regulars hold them to it.

Meatball pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. In Rehoboth, The Crust and Craft would not serve it any other way.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. Rehoboth has other pies; The Crust and Craft stakes its name on this one.

The Crust and Craft is filed in the Wood-fired chapter, under Rehoboth, DE. Menus change; the record keeps the signature as it stands.
