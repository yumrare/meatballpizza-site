Timber Pizza Company

Home: Washington, District of Columbia
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled, Petworth wood oven
Signature pie: Meatball pie

The directory files Timber Pizza Company of Washington, DC under Wood-fired — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball pie.

It is the standard Timber Pizza Company was built on in Washington, and the one its regulars enforce. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. It is the Timber Pizza Company way in Washington, and the regulars hold them to it.

The meatball itself comes crumbled, Petworth wood oven: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. That is how Timber Pizza Company keeps it in Washington.

The pie to know is Meatball pie. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. Washington has other pies; Timber Pizza Company stakes its name on this one.

Shops like this one hold a neighborhood together: the birthday pie, the Friday pie, the pie after the game. The meatball version is the connoisseur's standing order among them. It is the Timber Pizza Company way in Washington, and the regulars hold them to it.

Timber Pizza Company is filed in the Wood-fired chapter, under Washington, DC. Menus change; the record keeps the signature as it stands.
