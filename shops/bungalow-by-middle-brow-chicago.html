Bungalow by Middle Brow

Home: Chicago, Illinois
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled, naturally leavened
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Ask regulars in Chicago where the meatball pizza matters and Bungalow by Middle Brow comes up — a Wood-fired shop whose signature, Meatball pie, earned this page.

At Bungalow by Middle Brow in Chicago, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. Chicago has other pies; Bungalow by Middle Brow stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is crumbled, naturally leavened — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. In Chicago, Bungalow by Middle Brow would not serve it any other way.

The pie to know is Meatball pie. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. That is how Bungalow by Middle Brow keeps it in Chicago.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. In Chicago, Bungalow by Middle Brow would not serve it any other way.
