Spinato's Pizzeria

Home: Phoenix, Arizona
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

Spinato's Pizzeria (Phoenix, AZ) keeps the Chicago tavern thin faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

At Spinato's Pizzeria in Phoenix, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. It is the Spinato's Pizzeria way in Phoenix, and the regulars hold them to it.

Here the meatball is crumbled. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. It is the Spinato's Pizzeria way in Phoenix, and the regulars hold them to it.

The pie to know is Meatball tavern pie. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. That is how Spinato's Pizzeria keeps it in Phoenix.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. Phoenix has other pies; Spinato's Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.
