Kailua-Kona, Hawaii · Directory Entry

Black Rock Pizza

Home: Kailua-Kona, Hawaii
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

The record of Wood-fired pizza in HI runs through rooms like Black Rock Pizza in Kailua-Kona, where the meatball pie is treated as the main event rather than an option.

At Black Rock Pizza in Kailua-Kona, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. That is how Black Rock Pizza keeps it in Kailua-Kona.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. Kailua-Kona has other pies; Black Rock Pizza stakes its name on this one.

Meatball pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. It is the Black Rock Pizza way in Kailua-Kona, and the regulars hold them to it.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. In Kailua-Kona, Black Rock Pizza would not serve it any other way.

That is the whole of the Black Rock Pizza entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
