Back Road Pizza

Home: Santa Fe, New Mexico
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Ask regulars in Santa Fe where the meatball pizza matters and Back Road Pizza comes up — a New York slice shop whose signature, Meatball slice, earned this page.

Back Road Pizza works to that standard in Santa Fe — and asks to be judged by it. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. That is how Back Road Pizza keeps it in Santa Fe.

Here the meatball is sliced — sliced before it meets the pie, which is the pizzeria's oldest trick for even cooking: no raw centers, no rolling cargo, just meat that crisps where the oven can reach it. In Santa Fe, Back Road Pizza would not serve it any other way.

Meatball slice is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. That is how Back Road Pizza keeps it in Santa Fe.
