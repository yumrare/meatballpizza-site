800 Degrees Pizza

Home: Fort Wayne, Indiana
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled, three fires
Signature pie: Meatball pie

800 Degrees Pizza is Fort Wayne's entry in the meatball pizza record — a Neapolitan kitchen in IN where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

800 Degrees Pizza works to that standard in Fort Wayne — and asks to be judged by it. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. Fort Wayne has other pies; 800 Degrees Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The meatball itself comes crumbled, three fires: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. In Fort Wayne, 800 Degrees Pizza would not serve it any other way.
