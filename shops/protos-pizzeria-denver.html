Proto's Pizzeria

Home: Denver, Colorado
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled, LoHi
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Proto's Pizzeria (Denver, CO) keeps the Neapolitan faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

Proto's Pizzeria in Denver is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. Proto's Pizzeria keeps that standard in Denver, night after night.

The preparation is crumbled, LoHi — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. It is the Proto's Pizzeria way in Denver, and the regulars hold them to it.
