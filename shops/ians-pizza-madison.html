Ian's Pizza

Home: Madison, Wisconsin
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, mac-and-cheese fame nearby
Signature pie: Meatball slice

The record of New York slice pizza in WI runs through rooms like Ian's Pizza in Madison, where the meatball pie is treated as the main event rather than an option.

Regulars in Madison hold Ian's Pizza to exactly that standard. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Ian's Pizza keeps that standard in Madison, night after night.

The preparation is sliced, mac-and-cheese fame nearby. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. It is the Ian's Pizza way in Madison, and the regulars hold them to it.

Order Meatball slice — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. In Madison, Ian's Pizza would not serve it any other way.

Shops like this one hold a neighborhood together: the birthday pie, the Friday pie, the pie after the game. The meatball version is the connoisseur's standing order among them. Madison has other pies; Ian's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

That is the whole of the Ian's Pizza entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
