St. Paul, Minnesota · Directory Entry

Carbone's Pizza

Home: St. Paul, Minnesota
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

The directory files Carbone's Pizza of St. Paul, MN under Chicago tavern thin — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball tavern pie.

That is the bar Carbone's Pizza sets for itself in St. Paul, pie after pie. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. Carbone's Pizza keeps that standard in St. Paul, night after night.

Here the meatball is crumbled. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. That is how Carbone's Pizza keeps it in St. Paul.

The signature is Meatball tavern pie. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. In St. Paul, Carbone's Pizza would not serve it any other way.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. St. Paul has other pies; Carbone's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

Carbone's Pizza earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
