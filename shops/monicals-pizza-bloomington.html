Monical's Pizza

Home: Bloomington, Illinois
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled, Central Illinois chain

Monical's Pizza works to that standard in Bloomington — and asks to be judged by it. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. That is how Monical's Pizza keeps it in Bloomington.
