Pete's New Haven Style Apizza

Home: Washington, District of Columbia
Style: New Haven apizza
The meatball: crumbled, DC's apizza outpost
Signature pie: Meatball apizza

Ask regulars in Washington where the meatball pizza matters and Pete's New Haven Style Apizza comes up — a New Haven apizza shop whose signature, Meatball apizza, earned this page.

That is the bar Pete's New Haven Style Apizza sets for itself in Washington, pie after pie. Apizza — New Haven's dialect is New Haven's dialect — coal-fired, charred at the edges, tomato-forward, with pecorino's salt over mozzarella's pull. It is pronounced 'abeetz' and defended like a hometown team. It is the Pete's New Haven Style Apizza way in Washington, and the regulars hold them to it.

The preparation is crumbled, DC's apizza outpost — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. Pete's New Haven Style Apizza keeps that standard in Washington, night after night.

Order Meatball apizza — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. In Washington, Pete's New Haven Style Apizza would not serve it any other way.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. It is the Pete's New Haven Style Apizza way in Washington, and the regulars hold them to it.
