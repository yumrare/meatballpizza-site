Homeslyce

Home: Baltimore, Maryland
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Homeslyce (Baltimore, MD) keeps the New York slice faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

At Homeslyce in Baltimore, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. That is how Homeslyce keeps it in Baltimore.

The preparation is sliced. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. Homeslyce keeps that standard in Baltimore, night after night.

Meatball slice is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. It is the Homeslyce way in Baltimore, and the regulars hold them to it.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. In Baltimore, Homeslyce would not serve it any other way.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. Homeslyce is filed under Baltimore, MD, in the New York slice chapter.
