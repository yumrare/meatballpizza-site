Glastonbury, Connecticut · Directory Entry

Luna Pizza

Home: Glastonbury, Connecticut
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Luna Pizza is Glastonbury's entry in the meatball pizza record — a Wood-fired kitchen in CT where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

Regulars in Glastonbury hold Luna Pizza to exactly that standard. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. That is how Luna Pizza keeps it in Glastonbury.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. That is how Luna Pizza keeps it in Glastonbury.

Meatball pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. It is the Luna Pizza way in Glastonbury, and the regulars hold them to it.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. Glastonbury has other pies; Luna Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. Luna Pizza is filed under Glastonbury, CT, in the Wood-fired chapter.
