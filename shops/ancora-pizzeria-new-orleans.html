Ancora Pizzeria

Home: New Orleans, Louisiana
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Ancora Pizzeria (New Orleans, LA) keeps the Neapolitan faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

Ancora Pizzeria in New Orleans is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. Ancora Pizzeria keeps that standard in New Orleans, night after night.

Here the meatball is crumbled. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. In New Orleans, Ancora Pizzeria would not serve it any other way.

The pie to know is Meatball pie. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. That is how Ancora Pizzeria keeps it in New Orleans.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. It is the Ancora Pizzeria way in New Orleans, and the regulars hold them to it.

Ancora Pizzeria earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
