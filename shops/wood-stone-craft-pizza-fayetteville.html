Wood Stone Craft Pizza

Home: Fayetteville, Arkansas
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Wood Stone Craft Pizza (Fayetteville, AR) keeps the Wood-fired faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

That is the bar Wood Stone Craft Pizza sets for itself in Fayetteville, pie after pie. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. It is the Wood Stone Craft Pizza way in Fayetteville, and the regulars hold them to it.

Here the meatball is crumbled. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. Fayetteville has other pies; Wood Stone Craft Pizza stakes its name on this one.

Order Meatball pie — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. That is how Wood Stone Craft Pizza keeps it in Fayetteville.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. That is how Wood Stone Craft Pizza keeps it in Fayetteville.

Wood Stone Craft Pizza earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
