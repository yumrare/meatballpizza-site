Rigazzi's

Home: St. Louis, Missouri
Style: St. Louis style
The meatball: crumbled with fishbowl beers nearby
Signature pie: Meatball pie

The directory files Rigazzi's of St. Louis, MO under St. Louis style — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball pie.

That is the bar Rigazzi's sets for itself in St. Louis, pie after pie. St. Louis style rides on Provel — a processed blend of cheddar, Swiss, and provolone that melts like no other cheese — over a cracker crust, cut in squares and rectangles. That is how Rigazzi's keeps it in St. Louis.

Here the meatball is crumbled with fishbowl beers nearby. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. Rigazzi's keeps that standard in St. Louis, night after night.

The pie to know is Meatball pie. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. That is how Rigazzi's keeps it in St. Louis.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. In St. Louis, Rigazzi's would not serve it any other way.

The Rigazzi's kitchen in St. Louis treats this as doctrine. Why does the meatball pie matter enough to anchor a national record? Because it grades everything at once. The grind shows in the texture. The seasoning shows in the first bite. The breadcrumb and the milk — or their absence — show in the tenderness. A kitchen can hide behind a fashionable crust or a rare cheese; it cannot hide behind a meatball.

Rigazzi's is filed in the St. Louis style chapter, under St. Louis, MO. Menus change; the record keeps the signature as it stands.
