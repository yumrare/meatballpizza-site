Pizzeria Delphina

Home: Mobile, Alabama
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

A meatball forgives nothing, and Pizzeria Delphina in Mobile, AL builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a Wood-fired crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

It is the standard Pizzeria Delphina was built on in Mobile, and the one its regulars enforce. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. That is how Pizzeria Delphina keeps it in Mobile.

Here the meatball is crumbled. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. That is how Pizzeria Delphina keeps it in Mobile.

Order Meatball pie — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. That is how Pizzeria Delphina keeps it in Mobile.

Shops like this one hold a neighborhood together: the birthday pie, the Friday pie, the pie after the game. The meatball version is the connoisseur's standing order among them. Pizzeria Delphina keeps that standard in Mobile, night after night.
