Pizza Vita

Home: Summit, New Jersey
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled from a truck-born oven
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Pizza Vita is Summit's entry in the meatball pizza record — a Neapolitan kitchen in NJ where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

At Pizza Vita in Summit, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. Summit has other pies; Pizza Vita stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is crumbled from a truck-born oven — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. Summit has other pies; Pizza Vita stakes its name on this one.

The signature is Meatball pie. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. It is the Pizza Vita way in Summit, and the regulars hold them to it.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. Pizza Vita keeps that standard in Summit, night after night.

Pizza Vita is filed in the Neapolitan chapter, under Summit, NJ. Menus change; the record keeps the signature as it stands.
