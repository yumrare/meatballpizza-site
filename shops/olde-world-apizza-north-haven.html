Olde World Apizza

Home: North Haven, Connecticut
Style: New Haven apizza
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball apizza

In North Haven, CT, the meatball pizza at Olde World Apizza is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a New Haven apizza pie judged by its hardest topping.

Olde World Apizza works to that standard in North Haven — and asks to be judged by it. Apizza — New Haven's dialect is New Haven's dialect — coal-fired, charred at the edges, tomato-forward, with pecorino's salt over mozzarella's pull. It is pronounced 'abeetz' and defended like a hometown team. North Haven has other pies; Olde World Apizza stakes its name on this one.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. It is the Olde World Apizza way in North Haven, and the regulars hold them to it.
