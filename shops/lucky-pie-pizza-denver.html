Lucky Pie Pizza

Home: Denver, Colorado
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

In Denver, CO, the meatball pizza at Lucky Pie Pizza is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a Neapolitan pie judged by its hardest topping.

At Lucky Pie Pizza in Denver, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. It is the Lucky Pie Pizza way in Denver, and the regulars hold them to it.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. Denver has other pies; Lucky Pie Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. It is the Lucky Pie Pizza way in Denver, and the regulars hold them to it.
