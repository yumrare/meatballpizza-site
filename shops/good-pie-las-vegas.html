Good Pie

Home: Las Vegas, Nevada
Style: Sicilian
The meatball: crumbled over grandma squares
Signature pie: Grandma meatball pie

The record of Sicilian pizza in NV runs through rooms like Good Pie in Las Vegas, where the meatball pie is treated as the main event rather than an option.

Good Pie works to that standard in Las Vegas — and asks to be judged by it. The Sicilian square is a bakery pie: a thick, olive-oil-crisped crust, sauce over or under the cheese depending on the house, and corners that regulars request by name. Las Vegas has other pies; Good Pie stakes its name on this one.

Here the meatball is crumbled over grandma squares. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. Good Pie keeps that standard in Las Vegas, night after night.

Grandma meatball pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. In Las Vegas, Good Pie would not serve it any other way.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. Good Pie keeps that standard in Las Vegas, night after night.

Good Pie earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
