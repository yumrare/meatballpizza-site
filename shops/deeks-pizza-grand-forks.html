Deek's Pizza

Home: Grand Forks, North Dakota
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Deek's Pizza works in the New York slice tradition in Grand Forks, ND, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

At Deek's Pizza in Grand Forks, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Grand Forks has other pies; Deek's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is sliced. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. That is how Deek's Pizza keeps it in Grand Forks.
