Independent Ale House

Home: Rapid City, South Dakota
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Independent Ale House (Rapid City, SD) keeps the Wood-fired faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

Independent Ale House works to that standard in Rapid City — and asks to be judged by it. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. That is how Independent Ale House keeps it in Rapid City.

Here the meatball is crumbled. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. Independent Ale House keeps that standard in Rapid City, night after night.

Order Meatball pie — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. It is the Independent Ale House way in Rapid City, and the regulars hold them to it.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. That is how Independent Ale House keeps it in Rapid City.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. Independent Ale House is filed under Rapid City, SD, in the Wood-fired chapter.
