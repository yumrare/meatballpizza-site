Hollywood Pies

Home: Los Angeles, California
Style: Chicago deep dish
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball deep dish

Hollywood Pies works in the Chicago deep dish tradition in Los Angeles, CA, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

At Hollywood Pies in Los Angeles, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. Deep dish is a construction, not a crust with ambitions: a buttery pan walls in layers of cheese and filling under chunky tomato, and it is eaten with a knife and fork without apology. In Los Angeles, Hollywood Pies would not serve it any other way.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. In Los Angeles, Hollywood Pies would not serve it any other way.

The pie to know is Meatball deep dish. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. Los Angeles has other pies; Hollywood Pies stakes its name on this one.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. In Los Angeles, Hollywood Pies would not serve it any other way.
