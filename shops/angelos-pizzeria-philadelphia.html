Angelo's Pizzeria

Home: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced with cheesesteak fame nearby
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Angelo's Pizzeria is Philadelphia's entry in the meatball pizza record — a New York slice kitchen in PA where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

It is the standard Angelo's Pizzeria was built on in Philadelphia, and the one its regulars enforce. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Philadelphia has other pies; Angelo's Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is sliced with cheesesteak fame nearby. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. That is how Angelo's Pizzeria keeps it in Philadelphia.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. In Philadelphia, Angelo's Pizzeria would not serve it any other way.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. Philadelphia has other pies; Angelo's Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.

That is the whole of the Angelo's Pizzeria entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
