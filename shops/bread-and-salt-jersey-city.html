Bread and Salt

Home: Jersey City, New Jersey
Style: Roman
The meatball: crumbled over al taglio squares
Signature pie: Meatball al taglio

In Jersey City, NJ, the meatball pizza at Bread and Salt is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a Roman pie judged by its hardest topping.

Bread and Salt works to that standard in Jersey City — and asks to be judged by it. Roman style is the baker's pizza — long-fermented, cut with scissors, sold al taglio, with a crust full of air and crunch. Bread and Salt keeps that standard in Jersey City, night after night.

Here the meatball is crumbled over al taglio squares. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. It is the Bread and Salt way in Jersey City, and the regulars hold them to it.

Order Meatball al taglio — the house meatball pie, and the reason for this entry. It is the plate the regulars judge first and the one new cooks learn before anything else. Bread and Salt keeps that standard in Jersey City, night after night.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. In Jersey City, Bread and Salt would not serve it any other way.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. Bread and Salt is filed under Jersey City, NJ, in the Roman chapter.
