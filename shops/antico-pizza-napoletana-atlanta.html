Home: Atlanta, Georgia
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled, Atlanta's Neapolitan landmark
Signature pie: Meatball pie

The directory files Antico Pizza Napoletana of Atlanta, GA under Neapolitan — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball pie.

That is the bar Antico Pizza Napoletana sets for itself in Atlanta, pie after pie.
