Kailua-Kona, Hawaii · Directory Entry

Pizza Corner

Home: Kailua-Kona, Hawaii
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

The directory files Pizza Corner of Kailua-Kona, HI under New York slice — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball slice.

It is the standard Pizza Corner was built on in Kailua-Kona, and the one its regulars enforce. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Kailua-Kona has other pies; Pizza Corner stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is sliced. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. It is the Pizza Corner way in Kailua-Kona, and the regulars hold them to it.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Pizza Corner keeps that standard in Kailua-Kona, night after night.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. It is the Pizza Corner way in Kailua-Kona, and the regulars hold them to it.

Pizza Corner is filed in the New York slice chapter, under Kailua-Kona, HI. Menus change; the record keeps the signature as it stands.
