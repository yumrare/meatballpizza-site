Kraus' Pizza

Home: Canton, Ohio
Style: Chicago tavern thin
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball tavern pie

Ask regulars in Canton where the meatball pizza matters and Kraus' Pizza comes up — a Chicago tavern thin shop whose signature, Meatball tavern pie, earned this page.

At Kraus' Pizza in Canton, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. Tavern style is Chicago's other pie — cracker-thin, square-cut, toppings to the edge, built for a bar table and a long evening. That is how Kraus' Pizza keeps it in Canton.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. Canton has other pies; Kraus' Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The pie to know is Meatball tavern pie. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. In Canton, Kraus' Pizza would not serve it any other way.
