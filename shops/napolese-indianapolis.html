Indianapolis, Indiana · Directory Entry

Napolese

Home: Indianapolis, Indiana
Style: Neapolitan
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Napolese is Indianapolis's entry in the meatball pizza record — a Neapolitan kitchen in IN where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

At Napolese in Indianapolis, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. The Neapolitan school is a discipline: a soft, leopard-spotted crust from a fierce oven, San Marzano-style tomato, and restraint everywhere. Meatballs here are used sparingly, the way Naples would insist. That is how Napolese keeps it in Indianapolis.

The preparation is crumbled — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. Indianapolis has other pies; Napolese stakes its name on this one.

Meatball pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. Indianapolis has other pies; Napolese stakes its name on this one.

Shops like this one hold a neighborhood together: the birthday pie, the Friday pie, the pie after the game. The meatball version is the connoisseur's standing order among them. In Indianapolis, Napolese would not serve it any other way.

There is a reason Napolese draws its Indianapolis following. Why does the meatball pie matter enough to anchor a national record? Because it grades everything at once. The grind shows in the texture. The seasoning shows in the first bite. The breadcrumb and the milk — or their absence — show in the tenderness. A kitchen can hide behind a fashionable crust or a rare cheese; it cannot hide behind a meatball.

That is the whole of the Napolese entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
