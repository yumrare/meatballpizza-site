La Festa Brick & Brew

Home: Dover, New Hampshire
Style: Brick oven
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

The directory files La Festa Brick & Brew of Dover, NH under Brick oven — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball pie.

Regulars in Dover hold La Festa Brick & Brew to exactly that standard. The deck-oven tradition is the neighborhood standard — steady heat, a well-worn stone, and pies baked the same way for decades. La Festa Brick & Brew keeps that standard in Dover, night after night.

The preparation is crumbled — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. It is the La Festa Brick & Brew way in Dover, and the regulars hold them to it.

The signature is Meatball pie. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. In Dover, La Festa Brick & Brew would not serve it any other way.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. La Festa Brick & Brew keeps that standard in Dover, night after night.

That is the whole of the La Festa Brick & Brew entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
