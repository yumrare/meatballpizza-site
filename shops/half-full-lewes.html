Lewes, Delaware · Directory Entry

Half Full

Home: Lewes, Delaware
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Half Full is Lewes's entry in the meatball pizza record — a Wood-fired kitchen in DE where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

Half Full works to that standard in Lewes — and asks to be judged by it. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. It is the Half Full way in Lewes, and the regulars hold them to it.

The meatball itself comes crumbled: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. It is the Half Full way in Lewes, and the regulars hold them to it.

The signature is Meatball pie. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Half Full keeps that standard in Lewes, night after night.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. Half Full keeps that standard in Lewes, night after night.

Half Full earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
