Puccini's Smiling Teeth

Home: Indianapolis, Indiana
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Puccini's Smiling Teeth (Indianapolis, IN) keeps the New York slice faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

Regulars in Indianapolis hold Puccini's Smiling Teeth to exactly that standard. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. It is the Puccini's Smiling Teeth way in Indianapolis, and the regulars hold them to it.

Here the meatball is sliced — sliced before it meets the pie, which is the pizzeria's oldest trick for even cooking: no raw centers, no rolling cargo, just meat that crisps where the oven can reach it. It is the Puccini's Smiling Teeth way in Indianapolis, and the regulars hold them to it.

Meatball slice is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. Indianapolis has other pies; Puccini's Smiling Teeth stakes its name on this one.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. In Indianapolis, Puccini's Smiling Teeth would not serve it any other way.
