Connie's Pizza

Home: Chicago, Illinois
Style: Chicago deep dish
The meatball: crumbled, the 1963 original family pies
Signature pie: Meatball deep dish

Connie's Pizza is Chicago's entry in the meatball pizza record — a Chicago deep dish kitchen in IL where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

Regulars in Chicago hold Connie's Pizza to exactly that standard. Deep dish is a construction, not a crust with ambitions: a buttery pan walls in layers of cheese and filling under chunky tomato, and it is eaten with a knife and fork without apology. In Chicago, Connie's Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The preparation is crumbled, the 1963 original family pies — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. Chicago has other pies; Connie's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The signature is Meatball deep dish. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Connie's Pizza keeps that standard in Chicago, night after night.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. That is how Connie's Pizza keeps it in Chicago.

That is the whole of the Connie's Pizza entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
