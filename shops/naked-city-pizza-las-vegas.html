Naked City Pizza

Home: Las Vegas, Nevada
Style: Buffalo style
The meatball: crumbled, Buffalo pies in Vegas

Ask regulars in Las Vegas where the meatball pizza matters and Naked City Pizza comes up — a Buffalo style shop whose signature, Meatball pie, earned this page.

It is the standard Naked City Pizza was built on in Las Vegas, and the one its regulars enforce. Buffalo style splits the difference — a medium, puffy-edged crust, a slightly sweet sauce, and cup-and-char pepperoni culture that meatballs join happily. Naked City Pizza keeps that standard in Las Vegas, night after night.

The meatball itself comes crumbled, Buffalo pies in Vegas: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. Las Vegas has other pies; Naked City Pizza stakes its name on this one.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. It is the Naked City Pizza way in Las Vegas, and the regulars hold them to it.

Naked City Pizza earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
