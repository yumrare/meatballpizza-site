Coletta's Italian Restaurant

Home: Memphis, Tennessee
Style: Barbecue pizza
The meatball: crumbled over the barbecue pizza original
Signature pie: Meatball barbecue pie
On the record since: 1922

The directory files Coletta's Italian Restaurant of Memphis, TN under Barbecue pizza — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball barbecue pie.

Coletta's Italian Restaurant works to that standard in Memphis — and asks to be judged by it. Memphis barbecue pizza swaps tomato sauce for barbecue sauce and puts the pit and the oven in the same bite — a Coletta's-born idea this city claims proudly. It is the Coletta's Italian Restaurant way in Memphis, and the regulars hold them to it.

The meatball itself comes crumbled over the barbecue pizza original: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. In Memphis, Coletta's Italian Restaurant would not serve it any other way.

A kitchen can hide behind a fashionable crust or a rare cheese; it cannot hide behind a meatball.
