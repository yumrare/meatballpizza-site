Ziggy's Pizza

Home: Wichita, Kansas
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, Clifton Square
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Ziggy's Pizza works in the New York slice tradition in Wichita, KS, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

Ziggy's Pizza in Wichita is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. It is the Ziggy's Pizza way in Wichita, and the regulars hold them to it.
