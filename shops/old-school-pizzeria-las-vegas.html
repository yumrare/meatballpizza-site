The directory files Old School Pizzeria of Las Vegas, NV under New York slice — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball slice.

Old School Pizzeria in Las Vegas is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. That is how Old School Pizzeria keeps it in Las Vegas.

The meatball itself comes sliced: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. It is the Old School Pizzeria way in Las Vegas, and the regulars hold them to it.
