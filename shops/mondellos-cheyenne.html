Mondello's

Home: Cheyenne, Wyoming
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced
Signature pie: Meatball slice

In Cheyenne, WY, the meatball pizza at Mondello's is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a New York slice pie judged by its hardest topping.

Mondello's works to that standard in Cheyenne — and asks to be judged by it. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. That is how Mondello's keeps it in Cheyenne.

The meatball itself comes sliced: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. In Cheyenne, Mondello's would not serve it any other way.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Cheyenne has other pies; Mondello's stakes its name on this one.
