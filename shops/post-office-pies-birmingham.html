Birmingham, Alabama · Directory Entry

Post Office Pies

Home: Birmingham, Alabama
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled, Avondale
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Post Office Pies works in the Wood-fired tradition in Birmingham, AL, and its meatball pie is the house examination: grind, seasoning, crumb, and crust all graded at once.

Post Office Pies in Birmingham is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. Birmingham has other pies; Post Office Pies stakes its name on this one.

The meatball itself comes crumbled, Avondale: the house meatballs are broken over the pie in rough crumbles, so the seasoning — garlic, parsley, pecorino worked into the grind — distributes through every slice instead of arriving in lumps. It is the Post Office Pies way in Birmingham, and the regulars hold them to it.

The signature is Meatball pie. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Post Office Pies keeps that standard in Birmingham, night after night.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. In Birmingham, Post Office Pies would not serve it any other way.

Post Office Pies earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
