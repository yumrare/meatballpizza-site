Home: Washington, District of Columbia

In Washington, DC, the meatball pizza at All-Purpose Pizzeria is the order that tells you what the kitchen is made of — a New York slice pie judged by its hardest topping.

At All-Purpose Pizzeria in Washington, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. That is how All-Purpose Pizzeria keeps it in Washington.

Here the meatball is sliced with Italian-American plates — sliced before it meets the pie, which is the pizzeria's oldest trick for even cooking: no raw centers, no rolling cargo, just meat that crisps where the oven can reach it.
