Bennidito's Pizza

Home: Spokane, Washington
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced with beer buddies nearby
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Bennidito's Pizza (Spokane, WA) keeps the New York slice faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

Bennidito's Pizza in Spokane is where that standard gets graded, slice by slice. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Spokane has other pies; Bennidito's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The meatball itself comes sliced with beer buddies nearby: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. It is the Bennidito's Pizza way in Spokane, and the regulars hold them to it.

The pie to know is Meatball slice. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. Bennidito's Pizza keeps that standard in Spokane, night after night.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. In Spokane, Bennidito's Pizza would not serve it any other way.

That is the whole of the Bennidito's Pizza entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
