Richmond, Virginia · Directory Entry

Mary Angela's

Home: Richmond, Virginia
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, Carytown
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Mary Angela's is Richmond's entry in the meatball pizza record — a New York slice kitchen in VA where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

That is the bar Mary Angela's sets for itself in Richmond, pie after pie. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Mary Angela's keeps that standard in Richmond, night after night.

The meatball itself comes sliced, Carytown: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. In Richmond, Mary Angela's would not serve it any other way.

Meatball slice is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. It is the Mary Angela's way in Richmond, and the regulars hold them to it.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. In Richmond, Mary Angela's would not serve it any other way.

That is the whole of the Mary Angela's entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
