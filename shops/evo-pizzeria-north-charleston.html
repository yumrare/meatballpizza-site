EVO Pizzeria

Home: North Charleston, South Carolina
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled, Extra Virgin Oven
Signature pie: Meatball pie

EVO Pizzeria (North Charleston, SC) keeps the Wood-fired faith — and proves it with meatballs, the topping that exposes a lazy kitchen faster than any other.

At EVO Pizzeria in North Charleston, that standard is not theory — it is the nightly measure. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. EVO Pizzeria keeps that standard in North Charleston, night after night.

The preparation is crumbled, Extra Virgin Oven — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. North Charleston has other pies; EVO Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.

Meatball pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. In North Charleston, EVO Pizzeria would not serve it any other way.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. North Charleston has other pies; EVO Pizzeria stakes its name on this one.
