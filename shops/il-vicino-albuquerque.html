Il Vicino

Home: Albuquerque, New Mexico
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled, house style
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Il Vicino is Albuquerque's entry in the meatball pizza record — a Wood-fired kitchen in NM where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

Il Vicino works to that standard in Albuquerque — and asks to be judged by it. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. Albuquerque has other pies; Il Vicino stakes its name on this one.

The preparation is crumbled, house style — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. It is the Il Vicino way in Albuquerque, and the regulars hold them to it.

Meatball pie is the signature — the pie that carries the house meatball at full strength, built on the crust the shop is known for and finished the way the regulars expect. Il Vicino keeps that standard in Albuquerque, night after night.

Shops like this one hold a neighborhood together: the birthday pie, the Friday pie, the pie after the game. The meatball version is the connoisseur's standing order among them. Il Vicino keeps that standard in Albuquerque, night after night.

The directory records menus and signatures, never a number on a tag — the pie endures; figures are weather. Il Vicino is filed under Albuquerque, NM, in the Wood-fired chapter.
