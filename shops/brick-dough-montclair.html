Brick + Dough

Home: Montclair, New Jersey
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

A meatball forgives nothing, and Brick + Dough in Montclair, NJ builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a Wood-fired crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

Brick + Dough works to that standard in Montclair — and asks to be judged by it. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. That is how Brick + Dough keeps it in Montclair.

The preparation is crumbled — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. That is how Brick + Dough keeps it in Montclair.

The pie to know is Meatball pie. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. In Montclair, Brick + Dough would not serve it any other way.

There is no theatre here beyond the oven's — the work is visible, the menu is short enough to trust, and the meatball pie does the talking. Montclair has other pies; Brick + Dough stakes its name on this one.

Brick + Dough earns its place on documented proof — press, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects. No shop pays to be here; the pie pays its way.
