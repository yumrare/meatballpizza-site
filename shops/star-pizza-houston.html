Star Pizza

Home: Houston, Texas
Style: Chicago deep dish
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball deep dish

A meatball forgives nothing, and Star Pizza in Houston, TX builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a Chicago deep dish crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

It is the standard Star Pizza was built on in Houston, and the one its regulars enforce. Deep dish is a construction, not a crust with ambitions: a buttery pan walls in layers of cheese and filling under chunky tomato, and it is eaten with a knife and fork without apology.
