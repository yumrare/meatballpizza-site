Este Pizza

Home: Salt Lake City, Utah
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, Sugar House
Signature pie: Meatball slice

The directory files Este Pizza of Salt Lake City, UT under New York slice — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball slice.

It is the standard Este Pizza was built on in Salt Lake City, and the one its regulars enforce. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. Salt Lake City has other pies; Este Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The meatball itself comes sliced, Sugar House: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. Salt Lake City has other pies; Este Pizza stakes its name on this one.
