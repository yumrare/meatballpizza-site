Boston, Massachusetts · Directory Entry

Pastoral

Home: Boston, Massachusetts
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled
Signature pie: Meatball pie

Pastoral is Boston's entry in the meatball pizza record — a Wood-fired kitchen in MA where the test pie is the one topped with meatballs.

It is the standard Pastoral was built on in Boston, and the one its regulars enforce. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. Pastoral keeps that standard in Boston, night after night.

Here the meatball is crumbled. Crumbling is the generous method: meat in every bite, edges that crisp against the cheese, and a pie that reads as a meatball pie from edge to tip rather than a cheese pie with guests. Pastoral keeps that standard in Boston, night after night.

The signature is Meatball pie. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Boston has other pies; Pastoral stakes its name on this one.

Shops like this one hold a neighborhood together: the birthday pie, the Friday pie, the pie after the game. The meatball version is the connoisseur's standing order among them. It is the Pastoral way in Boston, and the regulars hold them to it.

Pastoral is filed in the Wood-fired chapter, under Boston, MA. Menus change; the record keeps the signature as it stands.
