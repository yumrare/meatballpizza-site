Three Brothers From Italy

Home: Seaside Heights, New Jersey
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, boardwalk slices
Signature pie: Meatball slice

Ask regulars in Seaside Heights where the meatball pizza matters and Three Brothers From Italy comes up — a New York slice shop whose signature, Meatball slice, earned this page.

That is the bar Three Brothers From Italy sets for itself in Seaside Heights, pie after pie. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. It is the Three Brothers From Italy way in Seaside Heights, and the regulars hold them to it.

The meatball itself comes sliced, boardwalk slices: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. It is the Three Brothers From Italy way in Seaside Heights, and the regulars hold them to it.

The pie to know is Meatball slice. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. That is how Three Brothers From Italy keeps it in Seaside Heights.

The room runs the way good pizzerias do: a counter, an oven you can watch, boxes stacked for the takeout trade, and a rhythm that peaks at dinner and never quite empties. It is the Three Brothers From Italy way in Seaside Heights, and the regulars hold them to it.
