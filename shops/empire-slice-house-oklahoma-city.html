Empire Slice House

Home: Oklahoma City, Oklahoma
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, Plaza District

A meatball forgives nothing, and Empire Slice House in Oklahoma City, OK builds its reputation on exactly that risk: a New York slice crust under the least forgiving topping in pizza.

Empire Slice House works to that standard in Oklahoma City — and asks to be judged by it.
