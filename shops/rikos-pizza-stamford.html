Riko's Pizza

Home: Stamford, Connecticut
Style: Bar pie
The meatball: sliced thin, salad-pie fame
Signature pie: Meatball bar pie

The directory files Riko's Pizza of Stamford, CT under Bar pie — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball bar pie.

It is the standard Riko's Pizza was built on in Stamford, and the one its regulars enforce. The bar pie is a personal, thin-crusted round baked in a pan to the edge of crisp — a tavern pie meant to arrive beside a drink and disappear without ceremony. In Stamford, Riko's Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The meatball itself comes sliced thin, salad-pie fame: cooked meatballs are sliced so they lie flat on the pie, crisp at the edges in the oven, and give a little meat in every bite instead of a boulder in some. It is the classic slice-shop solution, and it works because the ratio never lies. Stamford has other pies; Riko's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The pie to know is Meatball bar pie. Everything else on the menu orbits it here; this is the order that fills the room's recommendation when a stranger asks what to get. Riko's Pizza keeps that standard in Stamford, night after night.

The clientele is the cross-section every great pizzeria collects: families on a Friday, regulars with a standing order, and first-timers sent by somebody who insisted. Stamford has other pies; Riko's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

That is the whole of the Riko's Pizza entry: a city, a style, a meatball, a pie. In this record, that is what a pizzeria is.
