Guido's Pizza

Home: Boise, Idaho
Style: New York slice
The meatball: sliced, Boise NY institution
Signature pie: Meatball slice

The directory files Guido's Pizza of Boise, ID under New York slice — and files it there because of one pie: Meatball slice.

It is the standard Guido's Pizza was built on in Boise, and the one its regulars enforce. The New York school asks for a crust that folds without cracking, a sauce with a little brightness, and cheese that stretches rather than pools. On a slice this size, a meatball has nowhere to hide. It is the Guido's Pizza way in Boise, and the regulars hold them to it.

The preparation is sliced, Boise NY institution. Slicing is a discipline: the meatballs are baked or simmered first, cooled, cut, and only then laid over the cheese, so the pie finishes them rather than depending on them. Boise has other pies; Guido's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The signature is Meatball slice. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Boise has other pies; Guido's Pizza stakes its name on this one.

Inside, the choreography is familiar and exact — dough stretched to order, the oven tended like a pet, pies called out by name as they land. That is how Guido's Pizza keeps it in Boise.
