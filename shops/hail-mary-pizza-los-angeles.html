Hail Mary Pizza

Home: Los Angeles, California
Style: Wood-fired
The meatball: crumbled, Atwater Village
Signature pie: Meatball pie

The record of Wood-fired pizza in CA runs through rooms like Hail Mary Pizza in Los Angeles, where the meatball pie is treated as the main event rather than an option.

It is the standard Hail Mary Pizza was built on in Los Angeles, and the one its regulars enforce. A wood oven cooks fast and uneven on purpose: the crust puffs, spots, and perfumes with smoke, and toppings must be built to survive ninety fierce seconds. In Los Angeles, Hail Mary Pizza would not serve it any other way.

The preparation is crumbled, Atwater Village — the kitchen treats its meatball mix as a topping in its own right, crumbling it over the sauce so the pie eats like a baked meatball parmigiana in slice form. Los Angeles has other pies; Hail Mary Pizza stakes its name on this one.

The signature is Meatball pie. On the menu it may sit among a dozen pies, but in this record it is the one that counts: the pie the kitchen's meatball was born for. Hail Mary Pizza keeps that standard in Los Angeles, night after night.
