Landmarks

Some ovens carry the whole history of the pie. This page gathers the 42 landmark pizzerias in the record whose documented histories stand in their entries — the first licensed pizzeria in America, the coal-oven originals, the inventors of whole styles — each with the record as documented. Placement here is earned by history, never sold.

On the record since 1903 · Boston, MA
Santarpio's Pizza — Meatball pie
Santarpio's began as a bakery in 1903 in East Boston and still serves its pies with house-made sausage and lamb skewers off the grill.
On the record since 1905 · New York, NY
Lombardi's — The original margherita with meatballs
Gennaro Lombardi opened America's first licensed pizzeria on Spring Street in 1905; the coal-fired margherita lineage every New York pie descends from starts here.
On the record since 1912 · Robbinsville, NJ
Papa's Tomato Pie — Meatball tomato pie
Papa's traces to 1912 in Trenton and stands among America's oldest continuously operating pizzerias — the tomato pie tradition in its original home.
On the record since 1914 · Utica, NY
O'Scugnizzo Pizzeria — Meatball upside-down pie
Utica's O'Scugnizzo traces to 1914 and serves the upside-down pizza — cheese under sauce — that the Mohawk Valley calls its own.
On the record since 1918 · Elizabeth, NJ
Santillo's Brick Oven Pizza — Meatball pie, Santillo's style
Lou Santillo set up in Elizabeth in 1918; grandson Al Santillo runs the great brick oven today, baking every pie to a doneness number the customer calls.
On the record since 1920 · Chicago, IL
Vito & Nick's Pizzeria — Meatball tavern pie
The tavern's roots reach to 1920; the thin, square-cut tavern pie served today is the South Side's reference cracker crust.
On the record since 1921 · Racine, WI
Wells Brothers Pizza — Meatball tavern pie
Wells Brothers has baked its thin pies in Racine since 1921 — a century-strong tavern table.
On the record since 1922 · Memphis, TN
Coletta's Italian Restaurant — Meatball barbecue pie
The Coletta family has served Memphis since 1922; its barbecue pizza — born here — once fed Elvis, and the meatball barbecue pie carries the same sauce.
On the record since 1924 · Brooklyn, NY
Totonno's — Meatball pie, Coney Island style
Jerry Pero, who had worked Lombardi's coal oven, opened Totonno's on Neptune Avenue in 1924; the family still runs the oven, and the pies remain resolutely whole-only.
On the record since 1925 · New Haven, CT
Frank Pepe Pizzeria Napoletana — Meatball apizza, Wooster Street
Frank Pepe began with a pushcart and opened on Wooster Street in 1925; his coal-fired apizza helped define New Haven's whole school.
On the record since 1926 · Boston, MA
Regina Pizzeria — Meatball pie
The North End original dates to 1926 and remains Boston's reference pie — brick oven, old-world room, and a line out the door.
On the record since 1929 · New York, NY
John's of Bleecker Street — Whole meatball pie only
John's has fired coal-oven whole pies on Bleecker Street since 1929 — famously, no slices, a rule the meatball pie obeys as strictly as any other.
On the record since 1932 · Louisville, KY
Diorio's Pizza — Meatball slice
Diorio's has served Louisville slices since 1932, one of Kentucky's oldest pizza names.
On the record since 1933 · New York, NY
Patsy's Pizzeria — East Harlem meatball pie
Pasquale 'Patsy' Lancieri, another Lombardi's alumnus, opened in East Harlem in 1933; the original coal-oven room is the one that counts.
On the record since 1934 · New Haven, CT
Modern Apizza — Meatball apizza
Modern has fired its brick oven on State Street since 1934 — the third point of New Haven's apizza triangle and many locals' first choice.
On the record since 1935 · Stamford, CT
Colony Grill — Hot oil meatball pie
An Irish tavern in Stamford began serving thin pies in 1935; its hot oil pie — stinger peppers infused in oil — became the bar-pie benchmark.
On the record since 1938 · New Haven, CT
Sally's Apizza — Meatball apizza
Sal Consiglio, Pepe's nephew, opened Sally's in 1938; the tomato-forward apizza and the long Wooster Street rivalry are both institutions.
On the record since 1939 · Brooklyn, NY
L&B Spumoni Gardens — Meatball Sicilian square
Ludovico Barbati founded L&B in 1939; its upside-down Sicilian square — cheese under sauce — became one of Brooklyn's defining pies, spumoni to follow.
On the record since 1943 · Baltimore, MD
Matthew's Pizzeria — Meatball pie
Matthew's served Baltimore's first pizza in 1943 from Highlandtown, on a cracker-crisp crust that still sets the city's style.
On the record since 1943 · Chicago, IL
Pizzeria Uno — Meatball deep dish, the 1943 original
Ike Sewell and Ric Ricardo served the first deep dish in 1943 on Ohio Street; the meatball version layers crumbles through cheese and chunky tomato in the original formula.
On the record since 1945 · Orange, NJ
Star Tavern — Meatball bar pie
The Star Tavern began pouring in 1945 and its thin bar pie grew into one of New Jersey's most argued-over greats.
On the record since 1946 · Detroit, MI
Buddy's Rendezvous — Meatball square, the 1946 original
Gus and Concetta Guerra served the first blue-steel-pan square in 1946 — Detroit style begins at Buddy's Six Mile corner.
On the record since 1946 · Baton Rouge, LA
Fleur de Lis Pizza — Meatball slice
Baton Rouge's Fleur de Lis has served its round-room pies since 1946 — famously cash-only for most of its life.
On the record since 1947 · Robbinsville, NJ
De Lorenzo's Tomato Pies — Meatball tomato pie
The De Lorenzo family began serving Trenton tomato pies in 1947; the Robbinsville room carries the crisp, char-edged standard forward.
On the record since 1955 · Hazel Park, MI
Loui's Pizza — Meatball square
Louis Tourtois, a Buddy's alumnus, opened Loui's in 1955; the Hazel Park room and its cheesy-cornered squares are Detroit royalty.
On the record since 1956 · Ocean City, NJ
Manco & Manco Pizza — Meatball slice
The Ocean City boardwalk institution traces to 1956 — the former Mack & Manco — and still anchors the boards' pizza argument.
On the record since 1960 · Rehoboth Beach, DE
Grotto Pizza — Meatball slice
Dominick Pulieri opened the first Grotto in 1960; its swirled-sauce pie became Delaware's defining slice, beach town by beach town.
On the record since 1964 · Brooklyn, NY
Di Fara Pizza — Dom's meatball slice
Domenico DeMarco opened Di Fara in 1964 and made nearly every pie himself for decades — scissors-cut basil, imported cheeses, and a line that never minded waiting.
On the record since 1964 · St. Louis, MO
Imo's Pizza — Meatball pie, St. Louis style
Ed and Margie Imo opened in 1964 and built St. Louis style around Provel — the processed cheddar-Swiss-provolone blend only this city calls its own.
On the record since 1971 · Berkeley, CA
Cheese Board Pizza — The day's meatball-style pie
The Cheese Board Collective has baked one vegetarian sourdough pie a day since 1971 — a single daily recipe, a line down Shattuck, and live music on the median.
On the record since 1971 · Chicago, IL
Lou Malnati's Pizzeria — Meatball deep dish
Lou Malnati, a Uno and Due alumnus, opened in Lincolnwood in 1971; the family's buttercrust and the Malnati meatball crumble are Chicago canon.
On the record since 1975 · New York, NY
Joe's Pizza — The classic meatball slice
Joe Pozzuoli opened on Carmine Street in 1975; the shop became the city's reference slice — the standard every other counter is measured against.
On the record since 1980 · Providence, RI
Al Forno — Grilled meatball pizza
Johanne Killeen and George Germon opened Al Forno in 1980 and invented grilled pizza — dough cooked directly over wood coals — a technique copied nationwide.
On the record since 1985 · Waitsfield, VT
American Flatbread — Meatball flatbread
George Schenk began baking flatbreads in a primitive outdoor oven in Waitsfield in 1985, seeding Vermont's wood-fired school.
On the record since 1988 · Phoenix, AZ
Pizzeria Bianco — Meatball pie
Chris Bianco opened in the back of a Phoenix grocery in 1988 and reset America's expectations of a wood-fired pie; the meatball version is as careful as everything else he touches.
On the record since 1989 · Morton Grove, IL
Burt's Place — Meatball pan pie
Burt Katz's Morton Grove room became a pilgrimage under Anthony Bourdain's spotlight — pan pies ordered ahead, caramelized crust, a lifetime's method.
On the record since 1992 · Chicago, IL
Pequod's Pizza — Meatball pan pie
Burt Katz opened Pequod's in 1992 after his Gulliver's and Burt's Place years; the caramelized cheese-to-the-edge pan crust is his signature.
On the record since 1996 · Anchorage, AK
Moose's Tooth Pub & Pizzeria — Meatball pie, Anchorage style
Moose's Tooth opened in 1996 and became Anchorage's living room — inventive pies, house brews, and lines in every season.
On the record since 2003 · Austin, TX
Home Slice Pizza — Meatball slice
Home Slice brought the true New York slice to South Congress in 2003 and became Austin's by-the-slice benchmark.
On the record since 2009 · San Francisco, CA
Tony's Pizza Napoletana — Meatball pie
Tony Gemignani — a many-time world pizza champion — opened Tony's in North Beach in 2009, running multiple ovens and styles under one roof.
On the record since 2011 · Austin, TX
Via 313 — Meatball square
Brothers Zane and Brandon Hunt began Detroit style in an Austin trailer in 2011 — one of the pies that carried the style nationwide.
Orlando, FL
The Meatball Stoppe — The meatball pizza, front and center
A meatball house first: the Orlando Stoppe built its name on family meatball recipes, so its meatball pizza is not a topping choice — it is the whole point.
