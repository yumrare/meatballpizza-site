About Meatball Pizza

Meatball Pizza is a reference directory: the pizzerias of America, documented by the meatball pizza — the one pie that tests a kitchen. Each entry records a pizzeria's home city, its style, its meatball (the blend and the preparation, sliced or crumbled), and its signature pie. 1,000 pizzerias stand in the record, across all fifty states and the District of Columbia.

The listing bar. A pizzeria earns its entry on documented proof: press coverage, awards, and the following a great meatball pie collects — with the meatball pie itself documented on the shop's menu or in coverage. Placement is earned, never sold, and no pizzeria can pay to be listed.

No prices. Prices rot; the record endures. This directory publishes menus and signatures, never prices.

Support. The directory is advertising-supported. Advertising never buys placement, ranking, or a kinder entry. The record is reviewed and refreshed quarterly.
