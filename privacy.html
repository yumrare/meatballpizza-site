Privacy Policy

This site is a free reference. It has no accounts, no signups, no checkout, and no comment system, and it does not ask visitors for personal information. There is no email address for this directory, no newsletter, and no form anywhere on the site that collects anything.

What is collected

The site itself collects nothing and sets no cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses and browser details) to operate and secure the service, under GitHub's own privacy statement. Tag management and analytics, where enabled, run under their providers' own published policies; this site does not receive or store that data.

Advertising

The site may in future carry advertising or sponsorships. If third-party advertising is served, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies under their own published policies; this site does not receive or store that data.

Links out

Where the site mentions outside sources, following a link takes you to that site's own privacy practices, which this reference does not control.

Changes

If this policy changes, the change will be posted on this page. This policy was published with the site in 2026.
