The meatball pie is the test. These 1,000 pizzerias pass it.

Meatball Pizza is the reference directory of America's meatball pizzerias — 1,000 pizzerias across all fifty states and the District of Columbia, each recorded by its city, its style, its meatball — the blend and the preparation — and its signature pie. No prices, ever: the pie endures; a number on a tag is weather.
